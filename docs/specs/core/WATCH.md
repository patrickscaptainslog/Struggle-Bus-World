# Watch sheet

One person who has never seen the game plays it. You watch and do not help.
We want to learn where a stranger has fun, where they get lost, and whether they want more.

## Before

- Link used: ____________
- Sound on, volume up. The mute switch was: on / off
- Hand over the phone with the game open.
- Say only "It is a game, see what you can do." Then say nothing, and do not point.

## While they play

Start a timer when they take the phone. Watch for up to 10 minutes, and stop when they put it down. Tick a line when it happens, and write the seconds.

- [ ] First paint (a tap that splats): ____ s
- [ ] First turn (steers by touching near the bus): ____ s
- [ ] First gear change (a flick up or down): ____ s
- [ ] Climbed (pulled down, and the bus went up): ____ s
- [ ] Looked around (touched away from the bus, and the view moved): ____ s
- [ ] Threw a Lob (a tap with the nose up that lands far off): ____ s
- [ ] Tried to paint something far away. What: ____________
- [ ] A shot or move they did not mean ("I didn't do that"). How many: ____
- [ ] First time they stopped and looked lost: ____ s. What they tried: ____________
- [ ] First thing they said out loud, and anything about a place ("that's my street"): ____________
- [ ] Kept playing past 2 minutes without being asked. Stopped at: ____ min
- [ ] Handed the phone back by themself (tick), or you had to take it (no tick). Their reason: ____________

## After

Ask these in order, word for word. Write down what they say.

1. What is this game?
2. What were you trying to do?
3. What was the best moment?
4. What annoyed you?
5. Would you open it again? What for?
6. Would you send this to someone? Who?
