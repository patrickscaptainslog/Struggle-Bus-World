# C15: whose paint a mover keeps

*A change to `docs/specs/earth/CONTRACT.md`, sections 3, 6 and 7, from the factions' step 1 (`FACTIONS.md`, section 5; decision 23: "Teach paint whose it is now, before the freeways and cars take paint"). Written on `feat/factions` for the first account to propose: it's Proposed once its `CONTRACT:` commit is on `feat/earth`, and holds once the second account's commit marks it Agreed (section 8). Until then the old text holds, and nothing in `src/life` is built on it.*

Marks as the contract's: **Agreed**, **Proposed**, **Open**; facts MEASURED (read or run on 2026-11-06, file and line given; lines move), SOURCE, ESTIMATE.

## Why

Every other paint store keeps its hitter's faction on `feat/factions`: the buildings, landmarks and bridges, the ground, and the Moon's. A painted mover keeps only its hue and level, so the cars, next in the moving things, would forget whose paint they wear (`FACTIONS.md`, section 5, "What changes now"), and a ferry or a cargo ship carries its paint for its whole journey (rule 8). Section 3 says "faction 1 (Rainbow)" for every mover today, and so the record has no room for another.

## The change (Proposed)

**Section 3, "Hue and level".** For "faction 1 (Rainbow)": the hitter's faction, by `src/game/factions.ts`'s numbers. A fresh mover takes the blast's hue and the hitter's faction at level 1; a painted one keeps its colour, goes up a level and takes the hitter's faction, at level 3 too, as a building and a crater do at their top (`Splat.ts`, `Splatter.update`; `CraterPaint.paint`). What a rival's hit does beyond that, and what a mover's faction looks like, are later steps' (`FACTIONS.md`, section 5, steps 4 and 6); this keeps only whose it is.

**Section 3, "What's saved".** A mover's faction is kept with its paint, for as long as the paint is (`until`): after a reload it reads back the faction that painted it, and a record kept before this, which names none, reads back Rainbow's, with its hue, level and `until` as they were (`ownerOf`, which every other store reads with). How the record keeps it stays life's, as the section says ("The record's shape and its storage key are life's"), and so does whether that takes a new version of life's store (section 8). Every other store on `feat/factions` writes a faction only where it isn't Rainbow's, so a Rainbow record is the bytes it was, and none took a new version. The caps can stay as they are: a faction is one small number on the 40 bytes a record is counted. `picnic:` and `car:` paint, the session's alone, keeps its faction in memory the same way.

**Section 6, `Life`.** The two calls that paint take the hitter's faction, last:

```ts
hit(key: string, color: number, utcMs: number, arrive: number, faction: number): boolean;   // true: a fresh mover took the hue
blast(x: number, y: number, z: number, r: number, hue: number, superTag: SuperTag | undefined, arrive: number, faction: number): void;
```

and the host's two lines pass it: `splatter.faction` at 871, and at 565 the blast's own, `ownerOf(b.spec.faction)` (the Splatter writes it on every blast it fires, but `BlastSpec.faction` is optional, `number | undefined`, so it won't pass as a `number` as it is). `Life.blast` passes it on to the `this.hit(...)` it calls for each mover in reach (`Life.ts` 789). Those are life's lines in `main.ts` (section 6), so life changes them with its own, and says so in `docs/TODO.md` first. No other line in `main.ts` or `moonrun.ts` changes.

**Section 7, L5** (`tools/life/paint.mjs`) gains two rows: each of the five factions paints a ferry through `__qa`, and after a reload at the same `?t=` each reads back its own; and a record written as today's, with no faction, reads back Rainbow's with its hue, level and `until`.

**No player id.** A record keeps whose faction, not which player: there are no accounts (`FACTIONS.md`, section 4, "No accounts"), a device's store holds its one player's paint, and the shared world's server, when it comes, keeps who painted what. A record's fields are named, so one can be added then without a new version.
