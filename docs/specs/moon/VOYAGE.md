# VOYAGE: the Moon trip as the producer wants it

The producer, 2026-11-03, after flying FREEFLIGHT's Stages 1 to 3 on `?moonland`. Their words are in `CLAUDE.md`, "The Moon trip". This is the plan for them. V1 is built, and V2 but for the street's flying at the Moon from orbit down. First picks of numbers are marked, and the producer can overrule them on the phone.

## In the order it's built

**V1: what's seen at once (second account, `feat/moonfree`).** Built 2026-11-03, waiting on the producer's flight: `DECISIONS.md`, "The Moon trip, its first part".

- No paint from orbit. The breath in orbit goes from `?moonland` (`MoonBreath`, and the big splats it leaves on `MoonPaint`'s map). Paint at the Moon is a crater at a time, as on the street. What earlier flights painted from orbit stays where it is.
- The pan in space goes where it's panned and stays there: nothing brings it back, not a flick's lift and not a timer. One swipe across the screen turns the view half way round (first pick: the screen's width is 180 degrees).
- The trail on the way is alive: it wavers, its colours run along it, it sparkles and throws a puff now and then, so the frame never reads as frozen. The speed lines stay.
- The bus carves on the way, with the right thumb, as it turns over the street: off its set path and back to it on the lift, the path itself unchanged (the producer's word after V1's first build). The left of the screen pans.
- The Sun is drawn bigger than true, with a glow (first pick: four times). The Moon is drawn bigger from far off and comes to its true size as the bus arrives, so it grows the whole way there. Earth stays as it is.

**V2: the trip's shape (second account).** The boost, both buttons and the welcome built 2026-11-03 and waiting on the producer's flight (`DECISIONS.md`, "The Moon trip, its second part"); the street's flying at the Moon from orbit down is still to do.

- One MOON BOOST, there or back in a bit under 6 minutes (first pick: 5.5). The gears in space become that one.
- TO THE MOON? comes up extremely high over Earth (first pick: 13,000 km, where the whole Earth just fits the view) and goes if the bus comes back under 10,000 km. Tapping it starts the boost. Until the launch from the street is in, the trip starts in Earth orbit as it does now, and the bus climbs from there.
- At the Moon the bus is the player's from orbit down, and WELCOME TO THE MOON says so: the yoke, the gears and their flicks, a tap that paints the crater under the aim, as over San Francisco. Today the low game takes over at 14 km; here it goes up to the orbit.
- Leaving, the same way up: very high over the Moon (first pick: 3,500 km, where the whole Moon just fits the view) TO PLANET EARTH? comes up, and goes on the way back down.

**V3: pinch to zoom (second account).** Everywhere, the street included: the producer, "Pinch to zoom everywhere". Two fingers moving apart or together zoom; one thumb still flies and the left of the screen still looks. A pinch has to be told from two thumbs at once, which the controls already allow, and the controls sims (`tools/controls-sim/s_gesture.ts`) are where that's proved. It touches `Input` and `CameraRig`, `feat/thumbs`' files, so it reaches `feat/thumbs` and main only with this branch.

**The launch from the street (second account since 2026-11-04, at the producer's word; it was the first account's).** From San Francisco the bus climbs with its gears off the city's map and on up into the flight's loop, the way the Moon's low game hands over to it, until TO THE MOON? comes up. It meets V2 there, so V2's button and boost are what it hands to.

## What it costs

V1 about a day, V2 three to four, V3 one or two, the launch two or three. Each ends with the producer flying it.
