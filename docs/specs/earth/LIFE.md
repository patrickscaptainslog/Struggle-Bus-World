# LIFE: the movers

Life's own spec, the second account's (`CONTRACT.md`, the line between it and the world, holds over this; `LIVING.md` is the plan). What life does inside: its worker, what it sends back, how movers are drawn, how they take paint. Changes here need no cycle with the first account, unless they touch what `CONTRACT.md` says.

**State.** Stage 1 started 2026-11-05 on `feat/life`, cut from `feat/earth` at 03d3181, `feat/earth` merged in again at 303718e. Built that night: the mover system (the pools, the specks, the worker, the level of detail), planes live through the relay and scripted when it's down, paint on a mover from a tap or a super's blast, contrails, the flight's loop over 15 km, and the tests below. People on today's tiles the next morning, in the parks on blankets that afternoon by the producer's word, the sidewalks empty again; cars on the world's road graphs (`feat/world` merged in at 1cad05e) the same day, with the first account's GPU review's m1 (people on 2 m cells) and its frames (M1). Ferries and sailboats on `routes.bin` and the graphs' open water after them. Next: ships once the ocean's lanes are in (`CONTRACT.md` section 1), and airports' approaches once `airports.bin` is. Facts are marked as `LIVING.md` marks them.

## The worker

A module worker, as `CONTRACT.md` section 2 says: `postMessage` and transfer lists only.

- **In:** `init` (the place's slug and origin, the relay's address or none, the weather, the place's time zone, and its road graphs: a region's URL, the regions `city.json`'s `life.regions` lists, a region's side), `lawn` (a tile's grass for people, below), then `tick` at 10 Hz of the page's clock: `utcMs`, the bus's place on the map (x, y, z) and in degrees (for the relay), and the radius to fill: 80 km from the street, the horizon from the flight. The worker asks the relay itself, every 10 s, and fetches the road graphs itself, below.
- **Out:** a snapshot, transferred, which the host sends back with a later `tick` to be filled again: the same two buffers all night (`smoke.mjs` counts them). What a snapshot's arrival makes is its views over the new buffer, about 1.2 KB at 10 Hz (`alloc.mjs` prints it): a transfer arrives as a new `ArrayBuffer`, and with no shared memory there's no other way to read one.
- **A row** is 16 floats: the mover's key hash (u32 bits), kind, x, y, z at the snapshot's `utcMs`, vx, vy, vz (m/s), heading, pitch, bank (rad), size scale, flags (live), the seconds its paint would last from then (`until`), and two spare. The host carries a mover on between snapshots by its velocity, so a 10 Hz worker draws at 30 or 60 fps with no step.
- **Keys** are `CONTRACT.md` section 3's. The worker hashes them for the row (FNV-1a), and keeps the strings for the movers in reach, so the host can ask for a key by its hash when a breath lands.

## How movers are drawn

Three pools, one draw each in the scene pass, one geometry each holding every kind in its pool: each vertex says which kind it belongs to, and the vertex shader folds away what isn't the instance's (scale 0). So cars and small planes share the middle pool's one draw, and ferries, sailboats and airliners the large pool's: a pool's every instance draws every kind's boxes, folded or not, and the large pool's instances are few, so a kind's boxes cost least there (`LIVING.md` put boats in the middle pool). A pool's model is a few dozen triangles a kind, made for pixel art at 6 to 40 px. Under 6 px the same model, which reads as a few pixels anyway; under 2 px a speck, one point in the specks' draw (`LIVING.md` rule 4: never a draw for something under two pixels). Painted movers keep a 1 px floor to orbit (`CONTRACT.md` 1.4).

Per instance: its matrix, its ramp (the grey world's rows, or `HUE_RAMPS[hue]` painted), its level (1 to 3), a glint (the aim's, a step up, as on buildings) and a surface id. A model's boxes can be lamps. At night, when the windows light (the live sky's and the held hour's `uLit`), a grey plane's lamp is its beacon, on the fin of its model and as its speck: the lamps' ramp an eighth of every 1.2 s, each its own phase from its key; a painted one keeps its paint (the first account's GPU review, n1: a model showed none). A car's lamps are its headlights, on at night grey or painted; under 14 px, where its model's lamps are too small to see, a speck at its nose is them, which its body hides from behind; a car's speck is steady, and drawn at night down to 0.3 px where by day it goes under 1. Each pool's material ends in `sbWrite`, flags 1.0 (outlined); specks and marks 0.5. All of them are registered with the warm-up (`registerWarm('life', ...)`), so nothing compiles in flight.

The pools are made at their largest at boot (`CONTRACT.md` section 2: people 4,096, middle 2,048, large 512, specks 16,384) and drawn with `count` set to what's live, one upload of the live range an attribute a frame, its range object reused (three's `addUpdateRange` makes one a call).

**Draws.** Five in all, each only while something in it is live: the three pools, the specks and the marks (the first account's GPU review, m2: `LIVING.md`'s three draws were the pools). By the code, a pool's instance draws every box of every kind in it, those not its own folded to a point: a picnic or a dancer 23 boxes, 276 triangles, a picnic's 36 to 180 of them shown; a car or a small plane 11, 132, a car's 72 of them shown; an airliner, a ferry or a sailboat 20, 240. So 700 cars at a morning peak are about 92 K triangles sent and 50 K drawn, and 1,200 picnics on a Saturday afternoon (the most the worker fills) 330 K sent, against `LIVING.md`'s 80 K for the whole of life (ESTIMATE). Not yet measured on any GPU: L3.

**Marks** (`CONTRACT.md` O6): one draw of ribbons, 8,192 vertices, 12 points a ribbon. A painted plane's contrail runs back along its path from its tail for as long as it has flown since the hit, 40 s of it at most, turned to the lens, a pixel wider a level up (2, 3, 4 px, and 8 m at the least), on its paint's ramp; at level 3 it runs through the hues. It fades as paint can in an indexed G-buffer, its pixels dropping out by a 4 x 4 ordered dither toward the old end, its first third solid, so the newest paint is the loudest. Flags 0.5, no shadow, fog cut as a painted speck's. A painted car's tyre tracks are two of them laid flat on the road from its rear axle back, either side of its middle, 0.32 m wide at the least and a pixel at the most, 0.12 m over the road: where it has been, a point every 2.2 m kept by the host for 48 cars at once (the snapshot has no path), each stretch its own hue round all six, the hue it was laid in, so the rainbow stays on the road where it fell and the oldest end dithers away first. A painted boat's wake is a contrail laid flat on the water instead, 0.15 m over it, from its stern back for as far as it has gone since the hit and 40 s of it at most: 30 % of the boat's length wide at the stern and spreading to three times that, in every hue at any level.

**From the flight's loop** (`CONTRACT.md` O4). Over 15 km the flight has the frame, and its own scene and render frame: the bus at the origin, a unit a metre, its axes in J2000 from Earth's centre. `moonrun.ts` hands life the bus Earth fixed with the render axes in that frame (`frameSky(dt, utcMs, ob)`), and the pools move into the flight's scene while it has the bus, back into the street's when the street has it. Each row is put Earth fixed once a snapshot, exactly (the map's projection, then the ellipsoid), and carried on by its velocity there; none is drawn behind the Earth (the line from the bus passes inside the ellipsoid); past 25 km a mover is drawn nearer the lens on its own line of sight, its size cut to match, since the flight draws to 30 km. A plane at the hand-over is where the frames before said it would be (`sky.mjs`). Not so a mover on the ground (open, 2026-11-05): two ferries tied up at Oyster Point, 12 km off, jump 7.9 px on the flight's first frame where the same guess between the street's own frames misses by 0.09 px; every plane holds under 6. Not found yet: the flight's lens against the street's at the hand-over, or the street's flat map against the ellipsoid, both of which show most on what's near and low. `sky.mjs` prints it and fails on planes alone. Off the map's sky, from about 400 km out, the projection is past what it's good for, and the planet's own sky is a later stage.

## What today's tiles have, before the graphs

The plan's step 5, "until the first account's graphs come, read what today's tiles have": San Francisco's tiles as they ship (MEASURED 2026-11-05, every `r_*.bin` read with the game's own `parseTerrainTile`): 2,433 tiles; a 2 m ground class each, SIDEWALK 27.3 %, PAVED 20.5 %, GRASS 18.7 %, YARD 14.7 %, WATER 10.3 %, FOREST 4.9 %, PATH 1.7 %, SAND 0.7 %, PLAZA 0.5 %, ROCK 0.5 %, RAIL 0.1 %; 28,161 named centerlines (2,727 names), 1,974 km of streets and 137 km of paths and plazas, each clipped to its tile with 2 m to spare; 120,545 trees; the 8 m obstacle grid (roofs plus 3 m); heights every 4 m.

What that gives life: the grass people picnic on (below), the parks' areas from the graphs. What it doesn't: a street's direction, its lanes and speed, a join from one tile's piece to the next (only the name and the ends), and no ferry route, terminal, marina or airport. So cars wait for the graphs' edges (one-way, connectors, `NO_CARS`), and every boat and ship for `routes.bin` and the lanes (GRAPHS.md, CONTRACT.md 0.6).

## People

`src/life/sim/parks.ts`, by the producer's word (2026-11-05, `CLAUDE.md`, "The living Earth"): no one on the sidewalks, and the parks an afternoon field day, groups on blankets, "more pixelated and noticeable, more fun", the picture of Dolores at its peak what a park looks like at its own. With the bus under 1,500 m the host samples the tile nearest it in 700 m that it hasn't yet, 4 rows of its 2 m cells a frame (512 queries of each, the ground classes' own cells) through the world's own `groundClassAt` and `groundAt` (which cells are GRASS, and the ground's height in each), and hands the tile to the worker, its two arrays transferred; tiles past 1.6 km are dropped. Once the tile's region's graph is in, the worker lays its PARK areas on the cells, and a park's grass is its 4 m blocks whose every cell is grass in a park: never a path, a court or a playground. A tile's slots are 0.016 a square metre of them, 240 at most, each its own block (a stride through them coprime to their count); a slot's group comes on a day by its hash, every one at a weekend and half of them on a weekday, arrives from 11:00 local over five and a half hours, stays one to four, and is gone by 19:30, growing in and out over 20 s: at 15:00 on a Saturday about a blanket every 14 m (`determinism.mjs`: 127 on 2.5 ha of grass), none at 09:00 or at 21:00. It lies on the slope, its pitch and roll from the ground a metre either side (Dolores is a hillside). The worker fills them within 800 m, 1,200 at most, the nearest tiles' first; keyed `picnic:<tile>:<slot>:<local day>`, its paint the session's only. Drawn from the small pool as one instance a group: a gingham blanket 2.6 by 2 m, its squares a step apart, and who's on it by its look (the row's LOOK, the model's slotted boxes): one to four people, two sitting at either end facing each other, one at the far side, one lying along it, sometimes a basket, now and then an umbrella; a little over life size, their heads a step darker, so a group reads from the air. Painted, it comes alive, LIVING.md's "stop, look up, wave, dance": its people get up off the blanket, which keeps its basket and umbrella, and dance where they sat or lay, a quarter bigger, turned to the lens, hopping 0.3 m about 1.7 times a second each a beat behind the last, both arms up over the top half of each hop (`Kind.PERSON` and `Kind.CHEER`, poses the small pool draws, never a row's kind). Not yet: a painted park drawing a crowd that stays, people walking in from the gates, frisbees and dogs, and Dolores's own wilder peak (the producer: "a bit of an exemption").

## Cars

`src/life/sim/cars.ts`, on the world's road graphs (`CONTRACT.md` section 1.2, `GRAPHS.md`). With the bus under 2,500 m the worker fetches the graph of each region within 2.1 km of it, two at a time, nearest first, inflates and parses it (`lifeData.ts`'s `parseRegionGraph`), and drops those past 3.5 km; each time the regions loaded change it builds the net cars drive, in the regions' order so every player's is the same (50 to 80 ms in Node for 22 to 24 regions round downtown). A way of the net is one direction an edge is driven: roads only, never `UNDRAWN` (the decks the first account is building, which stay hidden until they land and the graphs are baked again), `NO_CARS` or a track. The ways on from each way's end are the ways from that node, a portal's two copies one node, in the edges' id order, never back the way it came; straight on is weighted five times a turn, and a turn back past 120 degrees is all but never taken unless it's the only way on.

Each way has a stream of slots, its headway at the busiest hour its class's cars a km each way (motorway 36, trunk 28, primary 22, secondary 16, tertiary 11, residential 5, unclassified 4, living street 1.5, service 1.2, ESTIMATE) over its length and a car's mean life, 2 s at the least. A slot's car is born at the way's start at a hashed time in the first half of its slot, if the local hour's traffic says so (a share of the busiest, read between the hours: 0.1 at 3 am, 1 at 8 am and 5 pm, the city's zone from `city.json`'s `tz` through `Intl` once a minute), lives 45 to 90 s, and drives a hashed path from there, each way at its own speed: the graph's, 85 % of it, 95 % on a motorway, else its class's (88 km/h on a motorway to 26 on a residential street and 12 in a service lane, stops and lights in it). The graphs have no lanes yet, so a class's: three on a motorway, two on a trunk or primary, one else, 3.2 m each, right of the line on a two-way road and across it on a one-way. Its place at `utcMs` is its path walked for its age: a closed form, so ten minutes' steps end where one jump does (`determinism.mjs`).

A car is placed on the chord between the points 4 m behind and 4 m ahead of it along its path, at its middle, facing along it, its lane's offset the mean along the chord: so it takes a bend round rather than through its corner, a short stretch of a way's line or a region's split changes nothing, and a lane's change between two ways is a slide over 8 m. Its offset is as much of itself as the chord is straight, so round a hairpin it keeps to the line. Nothing jumps or snaps: at a 20 ms step the worst was 0.26 m past its speed and 0.09 rad a step over 469,000 pairs. It grows in and out over 1.2 s where it's born and where its life or its path ends (a dead end, or the regions loaded); in a tunnel it isn't drawn, so it goes into the hill and comes out of it. The worker fills every car within 700 m of the bus and fewer on out to none at 1.2 km (3.5 px on the portrait lens), each by its own hash, so the traffic thins into the distance and a car appears as the bus comes on; all of them under 1,500 m, fewer as the bus climbs to none at 2,500. At 09:30 round the Financial District that's 600 cars, at 3 am 60; a tick of them 2.4 to 5.4 ms in Node here. Keyed `car:<edge id>:<direction>:<slot>`.

Drawn from the middle pool: an axle's pair of wheels front and back, the body, the cabin a little back, two headlights, 4.4 m long, its pitch the road's. Painted, a car keeps the paint for the session only, and comes alive: it bounces as it drives, about 2.2 hops a second and 0.3 m high on its own beat, squashed as it lands and stretched as it leaves, its nose nodding and its body rocking, and lays its rainbow tyre tracks (Marks, above). The honk in the chord `LIVING.md` names isn't in yet. A car is taken on the line of fire alone, as a person is.

## Ferries and sailboats

`src/life/sim/boats.ts`, on `routes.bin` and the graphs' open water (`CONTRACT.md` section 1.2). The worker fetches `routes.bin` once.

**Ferries**, on every route in it, a route listed both ways only once: San Francisco's 20 are 19 lines. A timetable made from the route, "very educated guess simulated" (the producer): from 06:00 to 22:00 local, a vessel every half hour on a run under 5 km and every hour on a longer one, as many vessels as a round trip takes, 26 in all; each crossing at its run's speed (7 m/s on a run under 5 km, 12 on one to 15 km, 15 on a longer one: the bay's fast catamarans on the long runs), a minute and a half to get up to it and to slow, 8 minutes at each end, the first 2.5 of them turning round at the berth, round to its right; laid up at its first terminal overnight, facing out, and a round trip that started before 22:00 finishes past it, past midnight too. Its place and heading are taken on a 120 m chord of its route. At 09:30 22 of the 26 are underway, at 3 am none (`determinism.mjs`), and over a whole day none jumps or turns faster than about 5 degrees a second. Every ferry is in the snapshot whatever the bus's height, at the water's height (`WATER_Y`); a ferry is 2 px at about 19 km. Keyed by vessel, `ferry:<route>:<vessel>`, and kept (`LIVING.md`: "a ferry or a cruise ship by its vessel"). Drawn from the large pool: the hull, two decks of cabin, the wheelhouse on top, each deck's windows a lamp along either side, lit at night grey or painted; its speck is its lights.

**Sailboats**, out of each marina in `routes.bin` by day, more at weekends: a slot for every ten berths (111 in San Francisco's 9 marinas), every one out on a Saturday and 45 % on a weekday, from a hashed time after 09:30 for two to five hours, home by 19:00, growing in and out over a minute at each end (27 out at 14:00 on a Thursday, 65 on a Saturday). Each slot sails a loop off its marina, an ellipse 250 m to 1 km out, 120 to 500 m long, tried from hashed ones until one is all open water with a margin: the graphs' `OPEN_WATER` triangles of every region within 1.4 km of the marina laid on a 20 m grid, a point water only if its cell and the eight round it are, so every player's loop is the same once those regions are in (85 of the 111 find one). Round it at 2.8 to 4.2 m/s, heeled away from the bay's westerly, the most with the wind on her beam. A marina is sailed while the bus is within 6 km of it and under 6 km up, and its boats filled to 5 km round the bus; its regions are fetched for its water whether or not cars want them. Keyed `sail:<marina>:<day>:<slot>`, its paint the session's only. Drawn from the large pool: the hull, its cabin, the mast, the mainsail and the jib.

Painted, a boat rides a swell, about once in 1.6 s, its bow and sides rising and falling in turn, a metre a 40 m of it, and lays its rainbow wake (Marks, above). Not yet: a ferry's horn in the progression, its rainbow decks a hue a deck, a sailboat's rainbow sails and the regatta a painted bay starts, all `LIVING.md`'s; and a boat's way from its berth to its loop. Boats are taken by the aim's pick radius, as planes are, not on the line of fire alone.

## Planes

**Live**, through the relay: the game asks `/api/planes?lat=&lon=&r=` (a box round the bus, in nautical miles as adsb.lol takes it) every 10 s while the street or the flight is over Earth. The relay asks adsb.lol's `v2/point` and keeps the answer at the edge for 10 s (`Cache-Control: s-maxage=10`), so a thousand players over one city make one request upstream. What it passes on, per aircraft: hex, flight, type, lat, lon, `alt_geom` (else `alt_baro`), ground speed, track, vertical rate, category, and whether it's on the ground. Only airline flights are named (`LIVING.md` rule 7: a call sign of three letters and a number); the rest go anonymous. A live plane is carried on from its report by its speed and track, up to 30 s, then dropped if no report has come.

**Scripted**, when the relay is down or not deployed (a page on localhost, a pinned or stepped clock, the tools): flights straight across a disc of 150 km round the place, at cruise (9.5-12 km, 60 %) or a middle height (3-7 km), two slots a UTC minute flown seven times in ten, deterministic by place and minute, no airport needed: 22 to 39 up at once over San Francisco across a day, 30 on average (`determinism.mjs`). Few cross the city itself (one in an hour over its middle on the 5th's evening): approaches and departures on each airport's real runways come with `airports.bin` (`CONTRACT.md` O11) and fill the city's sky. Nothing on the screen says which a plane is.

**From the street** at 15 km and under, an airliner is about 70 m and makes 6 px at about 13 km on the portrait lens (`CONTRACT.md` 1.4's arithmetic), so most planes in a San Francisco sky are specks and the ones on approach to SFO models.

## Paint on a mover

`CONTRACT.md` section 3: the aim asks `life.aim(...)` each frame, along the aim's ray and then the nose's (2.5 km, so a plane flown alongside can be painted); a mover nearer than the building target takes the glint and the shot, and the press latches it as a building is. A picnic or a car (`KindInfo.onLine`) is taken on the line of fire alone, within its own size of it and 2 % of the distance, the line run on past the aim point (60 m at most: it ends on the obstacle grid, roofs plus 3 m, which stands over the sidewalk by a building) with no building between the mouth and it (`Aim.clearTo`, marched as a wall in the line is); then no building near the aim point has the shot from it, but a latched one keeps it. So a crowd never steals a building's glint, and one behind a building can't be had; `life.hit(key, color, utcMs, arrive)` at fire time takes effect at the impact. A MEGA, a hose's every impact and its closer paint every mover in reach of the blast (`life.blast`). A fresh mover takes the blast's hue at level 1, a painted one goes up a level, to 3. The record is the key, the hue and level, `until` and the namespace that placed it, in IndexedDB (O14), 2,000 records and 256 KB at most, the soonest `until` first out; under `?t=`, `?rate=` or `?fixeddt`, a test store of its own. A breath on its way when the flight takes the bus lands at once.
