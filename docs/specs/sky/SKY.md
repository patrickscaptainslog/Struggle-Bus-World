# SKY: the live sky of Struggle Bus World

The build spec, 2026-10-31, against `merge/osm` at `bc3106b` (line numbers drift), revised after an outside review (Appendix C). It settles `research-astro.md`, `research-weather.md`, `research-render.md` and `research-art.md`. Where this file says less, the report holds the detail. Where they differ, this file wins.

**Labels.** **[M]** measured, tagged by who ran it: `astro`, `weather`, `render`, `art`, `skeptic` (the outside reviewer), or `here` for this spec (scripts in `shots/local/sky/work/sky-spec-scripts/` and `sky-fix-scripts/` beside it; any older scratchpad path in the reports now lives under `shots/local/sky/work/`). **[S]** from a source; a link in a table is one. **[E]** estimate. Unmarked numbers are design choices.

**Nothing here ran in the game, on a GPU or on a phone when it was written.** Every frame cost is a count, and every image is a recoloured real frame. S1 has since run on the laptop's GPU, never on a phone: section 7 holds its numbers. Its first job is still a real number from the phone.

## 1. The idea

Fly the bus and you are under the real sky of that place at that moment: the sun where it is, the Moon at its true phase, the stars that are up, the weather happening there now. No time slider, no weather menu. Real city data does most of the work: low sun down real streets, a lamp on every real road, a real ridge for the Moon to rise behind. Sun, Earth and Moon are one 3D model, so the Moon you look at is the Moon you will fly to.

**Where the hours go.** SF 2027, 08:00-23:00 local [M here]: sun at 10° or more 65.8 %, -1° to 10° 7.1 %, -8° to -1° 4.4 %, below -8° 22.7 %. **Day is two thirds of play and must beat today's frame,** deep night is the next quarter, and dusk is rare and worth stopping for.

**Four moments to build toward.** (1) Lamps coming on in a wave at the real dusk while you fly. (2) Real cloud shadows crossing a painted block, sun glitter on the bay. (3) A first night with something lit: the Golden Gate's deck lights over dark water. (4) Pulling at the ceiling while the bus wheezes and the real Moon swings into frame.

**The one rule: the sky is true and may colour the whole grey city, but paint stays the only saturated thing, and paint keeps the day.** Its gates: world peak chroma under 0.75 of paint's median (worst mock 0.71 [M art]), no brown or taupe pixel, paint capped at step 5.45 outside a glint, nothing off the live palette.

## 2. Architecture

SkyClock (UTC) and WorldFrame feed `SkyModel.frame()`, which writes SkyState. SkyLight, SkyTable, SkyDraw and SkySigns read it, with Wx scalars at 1 Hz from `wxWorker`. Materials write through `sbWrite` as always; the resolve's sky branch reads uniforms.

All in `src/sky/`, replacing WORLD-TRAVEL's `src/game/Sky.ts`:

| Module | Job |
|---|---|
| `Series.ts` | Generated by `tools/sky/trim-ae.mjs`: trimmed astronomy-engine plus JPL planets. Delta T 69.2 s; the stock model is 6.4 s high [M astro] |
| `SkyModel.ts` | Pure TS, doubles, no three.js, no allocation. Sketch in `research-astro.md` §4 |
| `SkyClock.ts` | `Date.now()` + offset from a no-store HEAD at boot, `?t=`, `?rate=` |
| `Body.ts` | Radius, air, sea, knee, table set, second light, gravity |
| `SkyLight.ts` | Key (Sun, Moon, afterglow), knee, tick. Replaces `updateSun()` at `main.ts:66` |
| `SkyTable.ts` | Keyframe tables, OKLCH blend, air axis, `uploadRect` when a byte changes |
| `SkyDraw.ts`, `Lamps.ts`, `SkySigns.ts` | Resolve uniforms, stars, sky lines, snapped Sun and Moon; lamp `Points`, pools; live sign numbers |
| `Weather.ts`, `wxWorker.ts` | City file, METAR blend, bilinear sampler. CPU only in play |
| `src/render/skyResolve.ts` | The sky branch as a GLSL chunk, out of `PixelPipeline.ts:935-960` |

**Frames** [M astro]. EQJ (J2000, km, Earth's centre) for bodies and stars; Fixed (turns with the body) for observer truth, both float64; Render (+X east, +Y up, +Z south), float32 in uniforms.

`Observer = { body, free, pos, axes }`: true float64 km and render axes as a mat3; `free` is open-space flight in EQJ. The model knows nothing about latitude, poles, floating origin or tiles. A thin `WorldFrame` builds `axes` from lat/lon with grid convergence, equal to Δλ sin φ through PROJ in SF, London and Sydney [M here].

**Update rates.**

| What | Rate | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| `SkyModel.frame()` | Every frame; at G3 the sky turns 4.4 px a second [E astro] | 1 µs, 6 bytes [M astro] |
| Series window | 60 s of sim time, linear, error 1e-8° [M astro] | 158 µs, p99 1.5 ms [M astro] |
| Key tick | When the shadow tip of the tallest building in frame would move 2 game px, floor 1 s. A 30 m wall: 40 s at 25°, 7 s at 10°, 1.7 s at 5° [M render]; a 260 m tower 8.7 times quicker. On a frame where the camera moved 3 px. Held still: within 60 s above 20°, sooner to 10°, under 10° the pixel rule anyway, so shadows creep; 60 s at 5° would jump a 100 m tip 237 px [E skeptic] | One sun pass |
| Palette | At most 2 Hz, when a byte changes | 2 KB upload; blend 0.35-1.2 ms, numpy [M here, skeptic] |
| Wx scalars, METAR blend | 1 Hz | Under 50 µs [E] |
| City file, METAR | Each new run, 15 min | Worker |

**Four light vectors.** A sun that moves every frame flips 13.6 % of shadow-edge pixels per frame at 8 km from the origin [M render, CPU mock], so:

| Vector | Moves | Read by |
|---|---|---|
| `uSkySun` | Every frame, true plus refraction | Sky branch, `GLSL_SKY_LIGHT` in both its users |
| `uKeyDir` | On the tick, the true key | Sun pass, sweep |
| `uSunDir` | On the tick, key with the noon knee | Every material, as today |
| `uGlitterDir` | On the tick, the brighter of Sun and Moon above about 1° | Water only, free of key and shadow pass |

**Sky branch order,** all alpha 0, dither `dsk` only: gradient, Milky Way, stars, Sun, Moon, clouds high to low, haze, the existing halo and flash. Sun and Moon snap to whole pixels with `snapCam`.

**One model, street to Moon.** Only Observer and Body change: Earth fixed with air thinning to G3; EQJ past about 60,000 km; Moon fixed (`rebaseBody` at about 60,000 km [E astro]), no air, earthshine, 0 on the far side. Worst error 0.0031°, tested to 39,000 km from either body [M astro]. Bodies are ray-sphere tests in the sky branch, sorted on the CPU, so occultations need no logic and nothing at 384,400 km enters the render frame.

**Rules everywhere.** Height comes from `dot(uZenith, dir)`; never store an elevation. `uZenith` is radial from the body, drives key and table, is +Y in the city game and replaces `dir.y` now. The camera's `uUp` stays apart: in free flight it follows the bus's nose. The sky test becomes the clear flag (`sbAux(p).a == 0`): `z >= 0.99999` turns geometry into sky at 25.0 km today and would cut `?farfade` at 29 km [M render]. The far plane is one function of height. The far fade is `uFog.w`, an air factor.

**Why ECMWF.** At what a player could really have, over 9,051 reports, it caught low decks at POD 0.67, FAR 0.57, GFS at 0.53, 0.73, while 6 hours staler; METARs within 100 km lift deck CSI from 0.38 to 0.55, own report hidden [M weather]. No model sees point fog.

**System** (decision 5). One Cloudflare account, two jobs that fail apart, because METARs hold the truth up late in a run. A cron Worker relays METARs every 15 minutes ([30 s CPU, 128 MB](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/limits/)). A cron at :17 and :47 checks for a new cycle and [starts a Container](https://developers.cloudflare.com/containers/examples/cron/): 110 MB, 1-2 minutes [E weather], ecCodes, since rasterio's GDAL can't read template 5.42 [M weather]. It writes one file per baked city to R2: ±3° at 0.25°, all 11 steps to step 36, under a fixed name, so no manifest, and one fetch covers a day and a half. **Global packs wait for world travel:** G2 and G3 are locked and the soft ceiling is 520 m, so no player passes 2 km. A file names only the city, as `./city/<slug>/` does.

**Credits,** in MAP DATA (`Hud.setCredits`) and a new `public/sky/DATA_LICENSE.md`: every source above, changed data marked, no endorsement implied. ECMWF's sentences come word for word from [its notice page](https://apps.ecmwf.int/datasets/licences/general/). Add: "Sun, Moon and shadows are real. Walls are shaded for readability at midday."

**Cost** [E; prices checked today]. A session is about 52 KB and 3 requests. [Workers Paid](https://developers.cloudflare.com/workers/platform/pricing/), $5 a month, [includes](https://developers.cloudflare.com/containers/pricing/) 375 vCPU-minutes and 25 GiB-hours of container; 120 runs of 4 minutes on `standard-1` use 240 and 32, under $0.10 over. R2 stays free until 100,000 daily players, 13.5M reads, $1.26 with no CDN cache. Domain about $1. **About $6 a month, $8 at worst at 100,000.**

**When a feed fails.** The sky needs no network. Weather eases in over 60 s and never blocks the first frame. Two tiers: the latest file, then plain clear sky. There is no service worker (0 hits in `src`), so a cold offline start never gets this far; the real cases are R2 down with the game host up, or signal lost mid-session.

| Failure | What happens | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Decode job stops | Last file runs on to step 36, then clear | With METARs deck CSI holds .57 to .55 from lead 9 to 33; model alone falls .43 to .35, one valid time [M skeptic]. S7 scores leads 24 and 36 on more days |
| METAR relay stops | Weight falls to 0 by 3 h | KSFO 0.85/0.15 at 1 h, 0.53/0.47 at 6 h [M weather] |
| R2 or signal down | File from memory or Cache API, then clear | Safari wipes it after 7 days of Safari use with no visit; home-screen apps count their own ([WebKit](https://webkit.org/blog/10218/full-third-party-cookie-blocking-and-more/)) |
| Mirror down (503 bursts [M weather]); GEFS down | Retry at the next check; smoke and dust 0 | |
| Slow connection | Behind tiles; clear until it lands | 50 KB is under 2 s at 30 KB/s [E] |
| New run arrives | Enters at the next step boundary; METAR eases over 90 s | Two runs differ by 9.8 points on average [M weather] |

## 4. How each thing looks and is built

**Time-of-day keys,** by sun height `h`. Light starts from art's frames and render's keys [E as final]; `a` ambient, `b` sky bounce, `k` key.

| Key | `h` | a / b / k | Key vector | Shadows | Table |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Noon | 35° and up | .30 / .22 / .46 | Sun with knee | Pass | noon |
| Day, setting | 22° | .30 / .24 / .52 | Sun | Pass | **today's bytes** |
| Morning, rising | 16° | .33 / .22 / .50 | Sun | Pass | morning |
| Golden, setting | 7° | .31 / .12 / .66 | Sun | Pass, sweep | golden |
| Sunset | 1.5° | .34 / .10 / .62 | Sun | Sweep | sunset |
| Dawn | 1° | .36 / .12 / .46 | Sun | Sweep | dawn |
| Blue hour | -5° | .34 / .20 / .30 → .06 | Afterglow: sun's azimuth, held at +8° | None | blue |
| Night | -12° and down | .30 / .24 / .04 | Afterglow or Moon | Moon only | new moon, mixed to moon by `m` |

`m = lit × smoothstep(3°, 12°, moonEl)`. Rising and setting tables cross-fade over 3 hours around solar noon and midnight, so polar days never jump. A solar eclipse feeds `h` through `sunSeen`, so totality brings the night table and stars with no special code.

**Weather tables.** Overcast, rain, storm, fog and snow each get day, dusk and night keys, mixed over the clear table by a Wx weight. Art made one of each, so ten tables are new work.

**Blend in OKLCH by the short hue arc.** sRGB gave 16 bad swatches, OKLab 15, OKLCH 7, all on the tree ramp's top steps [M art]. A clamp drops a swatch that would fail the gate to neutral chroma: 0 bad on art's 24 pairs at 9 steps, median move 0.019 OKLab, largest 0.068 [M here]. Paint rows are exempt. **Every keyframe names every row:** art's cover 22 of 48, missing INK, BUS_BLACK, GLOW_WHITE, SIGN_GREEN, WHITE, GRANITE and BRIDGE_ORANGE. `palette.ts:3-6` changes in words: a table is this minute's colours, and the palette check reads `__game.sky.palette`.

**Air axis.** One table colours a whole frame, so from orbit a dawn table would grade the sunlit and night sides alike. Above about 30 km it blends to an ungraded space table and light is carried by steps.

**Rows.** `RAMP_COUNT` 64, +512 B. 32 NIGHT_SKY, 33 OPEN_SEA, 34 LIMB, 35 CLOUD, 36 DRY_LAND, 37 TWILIGHT, 38 LIT, 39 LAMP, 40 MOON, 41 SNOW, 42 FARGLOW, 43 RIDGE, 44 CLOUD_SHADE, 45 SKY_LOW (equal to FOG by day; lets the sky go black while haze stays bright), 46 SAND (stops borrowing SKY_WARM), 48 up for VERDICT's ice, regolith and far rows.

**First paint.** `body` and `theme-color` are a fixed `#808a94` and the table lives in the main script, so a night load sits grey about 11.7 s at 30 KB/s (352 KB, the brief's figure). The inline boot script gains a sun height: 319 B, worst error 0.03°, wrong colour band on 0.05 % of hours at most, seven sites [M here], from the default city's origin or another city's `city.json`.

**Traps** (`research-render.md` §1.5, §2.1). `kMatch` hard-codes the FOG row: pass base and slope from the table. The beam's core is SKY_WARM 6-7: keep them warm in every table. `sunR`, glint and water glitter ignore key strength: scale by `uLight.x / 0.52`. `skyWarm` and `seaShade` need a sun-height gate and a zero guard at the zenith (89.9° at Lagos [M render]). `sc.up` flips at 82°. The cull sweep's floor goes from 0.1 to 0.02. The landmark clock reads real local time.

### 4.1 Sun
**Look.** A hard disc, GLOW_WHITE 7 with one ring of SKY_WARM 7, TWILIGHT when low, at true size (8.6 px street, 5.3 ceiling [M render]). The sky is 16 bands in 4 rows (`uSkyRows`): far side SKY_LOW and NIGHT_SKY, sun side SKY_WARM and TWILIGHT, joined by a narrow dithered seam. Near the sun the bands bend toward their horizon colours; an overlaid glow looked like a target [M art, by eye]. Belt of Venus from `dot(dir, antiSun)`.
**Build.** Resolve only, a 3-ALU circle test. Refraction lifts Sun, Moon and stars by a horizon formula scaled by air density, 34′ at the horizon [E astro], so the game's sunset is the real moment. `light += uGlare * pow(dot(dir, sun), 64)` across silhouettes.

### 4.2 Shadows
**Look.** True direction and length, always. Low sun is the look: at 1.4° 93 % of the land is in shadow, against 7 % today, and 25-48 % of land pixels are out of reach of any table [M art]. **Light moves, tables colour.** The picture is the lit minority, so the sweep is scored on that.
**Build.**

| Key height | What runs |
|---|---|
| 12° and up | Sun pass as today |
| 3-12° | Street only (vista under 0.35): camera distance `clamp(0.55 * ext / tan(el) + 300, 1400, 6000)`, far `2D + 600`, bias a uniform in metres [E render] |
| Under 10° | Sweep fades in, full by 5° |
| 3° to 0.5° | Sun pass fades out |
| Under 0.5° | `shadowStrength = 0`, pass skipped (`PixelPipeline.ts:435`) |

**The sweep.** On each tick a worker sweeps the height raster along the sun's azimuth. Each cell keeps the top of the shadow volume carried in from upstream, leaving out its own building. The resolve compares `P.y` with a bilinear R8 fetch of `tShadowTop` and takes the key out of `light`: +1 fetch on surface pixels, under 10° only. Base and step are uniforms from each sweep's range: fixed 4 m steps span 1,020 m, less relief than Cape Town or Rio [E].

**Which height.** Roof where a footprint covers half the cell (ROOF in SPIKE's `class` plane), else ground. Plain max widens every caster sideways and eats the lit walls. Against a 2 m ray march, 16 m cells, land pixels only, six SF poses of 60,000 each, flat roofs [M here]:

| Sun | Land in shadow | Lit sun-facing pixels kept, mean (worst pose): max height | As shipped | Shadow wrongly lit |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 0.65° | 54-88 % | 41 (11) | 82 (29) | 19 % |
| 1.4° | 62-98 % | 76 (63) | 85 (63) | 1.3 % |
| 2.5° | 52-97 % | 75 (58) | 86 (65) | 2.1 % |
| 5° | 36-87 % | 77 (60) | 88 (74) | 6.0 % |

"As shipped" is the roof rule, the R8 raster, the bilinear fetch and a 3 m downward bias at 1.5° and under, gone by 2.5°. The bias wins back first and last light (Alamo at 0.65°: 18 % kept without it, 93 % with) and must fade: left on over max height it wrongly lights 5.3 % at 2.5°, 11.5 % at 5° [M here]. Over 3° the pass covers wrongly lit pixels near the bus; under 3° the sweep is alone. Agreement hides this: at 1.4° on Alamo all-shadow scores 93.4 %, the sweep 98.2 %; at 12° the sweep (68.4 %) loses to no shadow (73.3 %) [M skeptic], so it fades out by 10°.

**S4's gate,** on the real raster at 1.4°, 2.5° and 5°: mean kept 85 % or more over three or more poses, no pose under 60 %, under 5 % wrongly lit at 1.4° and 2.5°.

31-59 ms for 429,000 cells in numpy on this loaded laptop [M here, skeptic]. Input: SPIKE's `height` and `class` plus terrain and surround heights, so hills and the East Bay ridge cast. Output: at most 1024² at 16 m round the bus, two copies swapped, 64 rows a frame, so a sweep lands in 17 frames, inside the 1 s floor. If ticks read as pops, anchor the shadow snap near the bus and let the sun move (render's 0.12 % row), behind a flag.

### 4.3 Noon
**Look.** Hard, short, graphic, its own state. Disc, shadows and glitter stay true; WORLD-TRAVEL's compression would make a 75° shadow 5.3 times too long [M render].
**Build.** `uSunDir` keeps the azimuth and takes a soft knee on elevation: unchanged to 28°, approaching 42° at the zenith, so true 75° shades as 41.4° and wall spread goes from 0.87 to 2.52 steps [M render]. Slew-limit shading azimuth to 40° an hour near the zenith. No knee without air. The noon table: cleaner, cooler top steps, a deeper shade step.

### 4.4 Night
**Look.** Paint burning against a dark blue city, windows and lamps waking, a real ridge behind. A moonless sky may go near black, because lamps and windows carry the frame. A light is a pixel. **A new player has no paint:** the real opening under art's new-moon table is a dark rectangle (mean L 0.279, 0 % paint) below a bridge in day salmon [M skeptic]. So the first night is lit by bridge, lamps and windows, and J1 judges it unpainted.
**Build.** Keep `uLight` mid-range and let the table carry the dark: a painted wall lands on steps 2.9-3.6, its strongest chroma [E render]. Paint rows are graded in the table: 90 % lightness and 112 % chroma at blue hour, 84 % and 110 % at deep night.

*Windows.* One stable threshold per window, never a timer: `h21(cell + bIdx) < want`, with `uLitHome` and `uLitWork` from local civil time. That needs `city.tz`, an IANA zone written at bake by [tz-lookup](https://www.npmjs.com/package/tz-lookup) (CC0) and read with `Intl`: no city.json has one, and `UTC + lon/15` is 1.67 h off in Amsterdam in summer [M skeptic]. Distance decides the form, because single lit pixels on a far tower turn to static [M render, by eye]: real panes at 2 × 3 px or more, whole floors on ribbons (`mppB > 0.6`), lit lines through `rhythm()` at least 4 px apart on towers past the pane gate, one tone per face past `mppB > 1.15`. Keep `share × glow ≤ 0.06` on far surfaces: 8 % of pixels lit at glow 1 boils 84 % of the area [M render].

*Lamps.* The street data is the night look. `tools/bake/lamps.py` walks the bake's road list, every drivable class but `service`, not the sign's names: named share is 99.2 % in SF, 72.9 % Ikeja, 49.2 % Mirpur, 29.0 % Gulu [M skeptic, four boxes]. A lamp every 38 m, alternating sides, 7 m off centre, `u16 x, z` on a 2 m grid per 4 km chunk: 50,803 in SF [M here]. Height comes from far.bin in a worker at load, 0 bytes against +66 % stored: within 4 m on 94.3 % of SF's lamps, worst 19.6 m, under 2 m in London and Amsterdam [M here]. One `Points` draw for the whole city, LAMP row, one pixel, depth tested, thresholded by hash so they come on in waves near sun -2° to -4°. Over 100,000 lamps, one draw per 8 km chunk. This list carries the far band too: lamps hashed from far.bin's PAVED cells lose the avenues [M here, by eye].

*Bridges.* Every span the game draws gets LAMP pixels along its deck at deck height and one on each tower top: SF's two today, VERDICT's OSM spans later.

*Pools.* Main stamps a detail tile once it holds both tile and lamps, one tile a frame: each lamp's pool, up to 11 m with hashed radius, into the top 4 bits of `tr.cls`, because classes stop at 10 (`tiles.ts:22`), then a 16 KB re-upload. One path serves a night cold load and dusk falling on tiles already up. Terrain lifts PAVED, SIDEWALK, PLAZA and PATH a step or two under `uLamp`: +12 ALU at night [E render]. CPU readers of `cls` mask `& 15`.

*Headlight and glow.* Beam 42 → about 90 m, the 2.6 at `PixelPipeline.ts:1163` a uniform, inside the night table. Raise `uGlassGlow` and `uCrownGlow` at dusk, resting surfaces at 0.55 or under. Glass flashes at golden hour from a reflect test on `uKeyDir`.

### 4.5 Moon
**Look.** A real lit sphere: exact position, phase, maria and tilt, at a 15 px floor (true 8.0-9.1 px street, 5.1-5.8 ceiling [M astro]). Crescents carry earthshine one or two steps above the sky. A day Moon is pale.
**Build.** Resolve. No phase, tilt or libration maths: `n = x·right + y·up − z·d`, lit where `dot(n, s) > 0`, map through `moonRot` from `RotationAxis`. Radius is `max(trueR, floorR × release)`, `release = smoothstep(6°, 12°, elongation)`, so eclipses are exact; within 6° of the Sun the Moon is under 0.27 % lit, so nobody sees it shrink [M here]. Past 40 px switch to ray-sphere. The best map is 4 times disc width [M astro].

Earthshine: `step = base + round(k · Φ(α) · tone)`. Moonlight takes the key above 8° and 25 % lit, `k = min(1.5, 0.22 · lit / max(sin el, 0.15))`; cross the vector only while `shadowStrength` is 0. The Moon is down for half of dark hours [M render], so moonless night must stand alone.

*Lunar eclipse.* Shadow radius 6,459 km [M astro]. Rose at the rim to violet at the core, never copper. Moonlight falls with the umbra, so the city darkens with it.

### 4.6 Stars
**Look.** One pixel, five tiers, no glow, a small dim cross brighter than V 2.5, colour only on the brightest. 61 stars read as calm in `orion-lookup.png` [M art].
**Build.** One `THREE.Points` sorted by magnitude, `setDrawRange` as the limit, `z = w`, rotated by `starRot` so it works from any body. A flag byte lets the sky branch read stars back (+1 fetch) so clouds and the Moon cover them. Crosses are 4 extra points, no `discard`.

Positions are exact. Which ones show is art: `limit = V(height) + dark − moon`. V is 5.0 at street, 5.5 at the ceiling, 6.5 from 10 km; `dark` adds up to 1 where built cover is low; `moon` takes up to 1.5 at full; it opens with sun depression: planets from -2°, V 1 by -5°, V 4 by -10°, full by -14° [E]. Extinction `k = 0.05`: the real 0.2 leaves one star in the street band [M astro]. A street frame holds about 5 stars, a 38° look-up 76-97 [M astro], and the opening's low camera 23.5° of sky [M render], so a night opening has them with no look-up.

Twinkle: chance of ±1 step per 12 fps tick `min(0.25, 0.05 · X^1.75 · exp(−h / 8 km))`, faded to zero above about 15° altitude, zero above 30 km, planets at 0.1 of that. Without the fade a look-up runs about 100 single-pixel flips a second [E skeptic]. Judge it in motion.

Milky Way: the ESO map when `limit ≥ 6` and the Moon is low, one fetch per dark sky pixel, 2 steps of NIGHT_SKY.

### 4.7 Constellations
**Look.** Only in the look-up, at night, clear sky. 1 px, 3 on 1 off, stopping 3 px short of each star, GLOW_WHITE step 1, stippled in over 1.5 s. One name at a time, nearest screen centre.
**Build.** One `LineSegments` of 680 segments, u16 star indices; gap pixels write the clear value, no `discard`. Same program as bolts. Other cultures are not v1.

### 4.8 Clouds
**Look.** Real amount, real drift, invented shapes. Foreshortening gives long streaks for free: a 5 km cell at 3° is about 160 × 9 px [E render]. Lit edges and warm undersides at low sun. Cloud shadows crossing a painted block are the day's hero. No dither inside weather gradients.
**Build.** Three ray-plane layers at 1.5, 4.5 and 9 km, each one tiling 64² R8 noise thresholded by cover, plus one fetch toward a low sun. Write them as one function with a depth compare that both resolve branches can call: the sky branch returns before surface code (`PixelPipeline.ts:935`), so from above clouds would show only on empty pixels. **No weather texture reaches the GPU in play:** Wx sends `low, mid, high` and a 16-entry `uWxRing`, cover 60 km out by azimuth, so a real front stands on the right side of the sky for no fetch [E]. `?wx=` presets drive the same scalars, so clouds ship before any pipeline. Cloud shadows use the same noise, offset along the sun (+5.3 % fetches, broken cloud only [M render]). Under full overcast `shadowStrength = 0`, so overcast pays for itself. While the deck slab is active the low layer is 0 for a camera above it, or climbing out ends under the same cloud. `?view=orbit` reads one static global frame. MAGIC-SPEC's `uSkyWarm[32]` (planned, not in the code yet) joins as `warmS = max(sunTerm, lut)`.

### 4.9 Rain
**Look.** Cosy, wet, lamps on early. Wet streets carry the picture. The honest signal is "showers around": 85 % of model alarms at Lagos had no rain at the airport [M weather], so rate drives chance.
**Build.** One `LineSegments`, 300-1,600 lines, motion in the vertex shader, wrapped round the camera, one game pixel wide, FOG row with the head 2 steps up, fill 0.5-4.8 % [M render]. `wetGround = 1 − exp(−rain in last 6 h / 1 mm)`. Wet streets: darker asphalt, sky fresnel, and 2 `tGlow` fetches up the screen, so windows and paint smear down the road. If 1 px lines fail on iOS, use instanced thin quads.

### 4.10 Snow
**Look.** Hushed and bright. Snow settles on what faces up, never on paint.
**Build.** One `Points`, about 900, 1-2 px, scaled by rate: `ptype` alone marks 25 % of cells with none falling [M weather]. Lying snow: up-facing grey pixels take SNOW in the resolve, +8 ALU, from `sd`.

### 4.12 Lightning
**Look.** A bolt, then a lift of the palette, never a white sky.
**Build.** `pipeline.flash()` with a lift mode, because `uFlash` whitens the sky at any strength (`PixelPipeline.ts:957`). Chance from `mucape × rate`: 0.41/0.92 at stations [M weather]; a METAR TS sets it to 1 nearby. At most 3 flashes a second, under 25 % of a 10° field ([WCAG 2.3.1](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG21/Understanding/three-flashes-or-below-threshold.html)), with a setting to turn flashes off. Sound in `src/audio`: a rain bed, and thunder delayed after the flash, gated by that setting.

### 4.13 Wind
**Look.** Paint moves, grey does not (`MAGIC-SPEC.md:81`).
**Build.** `10u 10v 10fg` scale painted sway (`uTreeShape.y`), glitter width, rain slant and puffs. `u v` at 850 and 250 hPa drift cloud layers at true speed.

### 4.14 Water

## 10. Decisions only the producer can make

1. **May the camera lift on its own?** The law says only the yoke moves the lens (`DECISIONS.md:67`). The ceiling pull is still the yoke and ships in S5 regardless; rest lift and the sunset beat are not. **Recommend yes to both:** without them stars are almost never seen. Blocks half of S6.
2. **Open a Cloudflare account, buy a domain, make one R2 bucket and an API token, turn on Workers Paid.** About $6 a month, and only you can create the account. **Recommend yes.** Blocks S7, nothing before it. **Parked with weather, 2026-11-01.**
3. **Sunbreak needs a new sentence.** MAGIC #7 says the unpainted city stays under flat overcast, false on a clear day and at night. **Recommend:** overcast keeps the spec; a clear day keeps the pool as warm steps in the table's day rows; at night the pool is paint's own spill through `uSunbreak`. One line now.
4. **Accept three small licence risks, and add them to VERDICT's counsel list:** rights in non-US METARs; ECMWF's "Service Charges" and service-agreement lines, which I read as not applying to a free mirror; Stellarium's share-alike on one data file. **Recommend yes.** None blocks a slice. **Parked with weather, 2026-11-01,** apart from Stellarium's line, which comes up at S6.
5. **Keep live weather off GitHub Actions.** One Action would carry everything, and [GitHub's terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-terms-for-additional-products-and-features) say "don't use Actions as a content delivery network or as part of a serverless application", though "a low benefit Action could be ok if it's also low burden". I can't tell how GitHub applies that. **Recommend:** a cron Worker for METARs and a Cloudflare Container for ECMWF, inside decision 2's $5. If Containers fail their first test, get a clear answer from GitHub or use a small scheduled container elsewhere. Blocks S7. **Parked with weather, 2026-11-01.**
