# M1: the door, measured

The studio's first milestone, 2026-11-14. The direction is `docs/STUDIO.md`; this is its first milestone as work.
Days are ESTIMATES. About 22 days across both accounts; playable around 2026-11-25 (ESTIMATE).

## What a player can do at the end

Open a bare link and paint the Golden Gate within seconds. Crown it and send its postcard. Fly to any street by name, paint a block there, and send a postcard that lands a friend on that spot.

## Where it is built

- **The base branch is `feat/sf`**, cut by the first account from `feat/handling` (the one link) with the work held on the laptop (`save-2026-11-14/wip-handling`). With no flag, its page runs the M1 set: the core's loading page and return visit, the door, the postcard. The rival buses are off. The Lift stays behind `?lift` until M2.
- The second account cuts one short-named branch per task from `feat/sf` (from `feat/handling` until `feat/sf` is pushed). Its Node tools need no browser. The first account merges.
- Nothing goes to `main`. The core goes live only on the producer's word, after its review (task 4).

## The two lanes, in order

- **First account:** 3 → 2 → 5 → 1, the first drop (M1a: the door and the bench, flown by the producer), then 7 → 9 → 10 → 12 (M1b), and the core's fixes after task 4.
- **Second account:** 4 → 6 → 8 → 11. If 8 is not in when the first account reaches 9, the first account builds it.

## The tasks

Each "done" is a check that fails without the work, a frame, or a number. Every browser check runs by real touches through the laptop's GPU lock.

1. **The bench link** (first account, 1 day). `?bench` flies a scripted 60 s: the door, one Lift (the `?lift` stand-in), a look down from 15 km, a postcard capture. *Done:* the page prints, for each leg, the median and 95th-percentile frame time, draws and triangles; a laptop row prints them. The producer sends one screenshot of it from an iPhone that is not a Pro. **M1 does not pass without that screenshot.** M2's floating-block scope is set from it.
2. **The map check** (first account, 1 day; moved by the lead: the door waits on it). A Node tool over the baked tiles. *Done:* for at least six start poses at the Golden Gate, it prints the distance and the seconds at Cruise (30 m/s) to the nearest square that can float (has houses, is not park). It names the pose whose first such square is closest, and says if none is within 60 s. Written into `docs/TODO.md`.
3. **The base branch** (first account, 2.5 days). *Done:* `feat/sf` pushed. The tree's major is fixed (a live blast leaves its own mark): its row fails on `wip-handling`'s build and passes on the new one. The rest row, the tree-seam row, `warmcheck` and `iosfloor` are green. The page with no flag starts the door.
4. **The core's review** (second account, 2 days; the first account fixes, 1 day). `feat/core` 4f33648..220cce2, the release candidate. *Done:* the review in `docs/reviews/`; every blocker and major fixed and checked again; the core ready for the producer's word.
5. **The door** (first account, 3 days; needs 2 and 3). The opening cut to about 3 s. The start pose from the map check. A glint on the deck in reach of the first tap. The thumb flies from the first frame. Hints at their moment: TAP TO PAINT first, DRAG after the first paint. The bridge climbs four stages (deck, cables, towers, lights), one counted hit every 5 s, with the ring; the fourth crowns it with a flash, a camera punch and the bell. The bridge signs GOLDEN GATE BRIDGE. *Done:* a scripted newcomer by real touches paints the deck within 3 s of the first frame and crowns the bridge within 35 s of it, tapping every 2.6 s. The first hint shown says TAP TO PAINT. With sound off every beat still shows (frames of the crown). The same row fails on `feat/handling`'s build.
6. **The link and save formats, first** (second account, 2 days). A spec (`docs/specs/core/LINK.md`) and a codec. The link carries outcomes (each square's stage and the order of its hits), not raw taps. The generation and a post's source go in the path (`/p/<hood>/<generation>`, `/s/<tag>`), where a page counter sees them. *Done:* a Node fuzz: 10,000 random states round-trip exactly; every loading order of 9 tiles gives the same saved city; a 17-square place's link is under 2,000 characters.
7. **Postcard v1** (first account, 3.5 days; uses 6). *Done, by rows and frames:*
   - At the crown, the card shows within 0.5 s of the hit-stop and holds until the next touch lands off it.
   - The picture is 1080 x 1350 px: white border, crew stamp with the short address (a placeholder until the producer picks a domain), postmark in Pacific time, the caption, and the question line (PAINT YOUR STREET NEXT; TAKE IT BACK? on a friend's street). Frames kept.
   - The share sheet is called inside the tap: the row stubs the share and fails if it is called outside the gesture. A file alone first; the link where the app allows.
   - Every card goes on a stack in the settings sheet, which also offers "send a postcard of here". The next visit's loading page offers the last card.
   - A postcard link opened on a fresh page lands the bus within 128 m of its square.
8. **The street index** (second account, 1 day). From the city's tiles. *Done:* its size printed (target under 100 KB, ESTIMATE); a Node check of 30 street names (Guerrero, Valencia, Hayes, Lombard, Geary and Irving among them) finds each on its own street.
9. **FLY TO A STREET** (first account, 1.5 days; needs 8). A text field in the settings sheet; the bus swoops to the street. *Done:* a row types five street names and the bus ends within 128 m of each, with a swoop, not a pop (frames).
10. **Real-line rows** (first account, 1 day). *Done:* the door row gains a weak-line leg (1.6 Mbit/s, 150 ms delay) and a 21:00 leg. It counts compressed bytes to the first frame from a preview build served compressed, and prints the seconds to first paint. It fails over 1 MB to the first frame (an ESTIMATE budget). If the build is over, the first account trims (nearest tiles first, the far field after the first paint) for up to 2 days; past that, slipping M1 is the producer's call.
11. **Neighbourhood names** (second account, 1 day; the first account bakes, 0.5 day). Cleaned in the bake's seed rules. *Done:* a Node check lists every name in the city's tiles: about 80, none on a deny list (road names, plaques), the Mission as one, Twin Peaks and the Castro present, and Alcatraz's square signs ALCATRAZ. It fails on today's tiles (BUNKER RD; Alcatraz says FISHERMAN'S WHARF).
12. **The check of `feat/sf`** (first account, 1 day; or the second account's review, 2 days, if the producer keeps today's review rule). *Done:* findings in `docs/reviews/`, blockers fixed, every row above rerun on the final build and printed in the hand-in. Then the preview link goes to the producer with the playtest below.

## The first drop's playtest (M1a: about two minutes)

**Open:** the `feat/sf` preview link, from Messages, with wifi off.

**Do:** tap the link and count the seconds until your first paint. Paint the bridge until it is crowned. Then fly on for a minute, wherever you like.

**Tell us:** the seconds to your first paint; whether you knew what to do without being told; one thing that looked wrong or confusing.

**Separately, when you can:** borrow an iPhone that is not a Pro, open the bench link, and send the screenshot.

## The producer's playtest (M1b: about five minutes)

**Read this on your phone. About five minutes.**

**Open:** the `feat/sf` preview link the first account sends you. Open it from Messages, with wifi off.

**Do:**
1. Tap the link. Count the seconds until your first paint. Touch nothing until the bus is there. Tap when the bridge glints.
2. Keep painting the bridge until the postcard comes. Send it to one friend on WhatsApp.
3. Open settings. Tap FLY TO A STREET and type that friend's street. Paint a block there. Send that postcard to them in Messages.
4. Send one postcard to yourself in an Instagram message. Open it there and paint once. Tomorrow, open the game in Safari: is that paint still there?

**Separately:** borrow a phone that is not a Pro. Open the bench link. Send us the screenshot.

**Tell us three things:**
1. How many seconds from your tap on the link to your first paint.
2. Did your friend tap the picture, and what did they say?
3. One thing that looked wrong or confusing.

**What we expect:** the picture may not be worth sending yet. The better camera, roofs and light come in M2 and M3. A "no" now is not the idea failing.
