# The grand idea: every bus is a piñata

## The producer's words (2026-11-18)

After the game's first outside player, a nine-year-old: he "loved the game. But I think he loved the shooting the other buses part of the game the most. He also wanted upgrades. Different guns etc. I think we need to do a deep dive and completely reevaluate the core nature of this game. I think we have something cool. But we need to zero in on what works. How do you think we should go forward and make this a super awesome viral kickass game that makes us money?"

The brainstorm: "Epic game of hide and seek / How long can you stay alive? / What's the city Dynamic? / Is hanging in the big City left for pros? Big buses? / Hunting down buses gives lots of coins? But so does having a long lifespan? / What benefits would starting of spending time in a major city possess? Why not camp out in Antarctica for a long time? What benefits does painting have? What benefits does hunting other buses have? ???"

Then: "Ok I'm thinking, you don't lose all your can at once, if someone hits you, they shake you a bit for some of the coins you have, but then they get a chance to run. But with better upgrade cannons, the percentage of coins achieved becomes greater each shot. Please help me develop a vision for the grand idea of this game. I want it to be as addictive as Fortnite"

The producer's coins idea is older than this: "painting gets you coins ... Maybe getting territory in a major city gains you way more coins that a desolate part of the world. Maybe hitting someone or shooting some other bus gets you coins. And you can use your coins to buy things" (2026-11-12, `docs/specs/core/COINS.md`, which STUDIO.md part 6 then cut).

## In one line

**Paint the town, fill your bus, and get it home to school before someone shakes it out of you.**

## The four ideas under it

1. **Every bus is a piñata.** A hit shakes out a share of the coins a bus is carrying. Nobody is knocked out, and the bus that was hit gets a few seconds to run. Better cannons shake out more. (The producer's rule. It is also Mario Kart's: a kart that is hit spins and drops coins.)
2. **The real world is the board.** Coins come from paint, so the money is where the buildings are, and the danger follows the money. Real schools are the banks and the safe places.
3. **Carry more, risk more.** A bus that stays clean earns faster and is worth more to hit. Every trip ends on one question: one more block, or run for school?
4. **Your bus is you.** Banked coins buy upgrades and looks. A crew, a livery, a roof pet, a horn, a dance.

## The rules, each with my pick

Every number is an ESTIMATE to tune on the phone.

| | The rule |
|---|---|
| Earning | Paint pays as COINS.md has it: a square's worth `w` from the volume of its buildings (measured over San Francisco: the Financial District 21 a square to the streets, the Outer Sunset 8.5, Presidio Central 4.4), each stage paid once, by its ledger. So a city pays more than empty land, and downtown most of all. |
| Carried and banked | Coins ride on the bus until it banks them. Carried coins can be shaken out. Banked coins are safe for good. |
| A hit | A shell shakes out 10 % of what the bus carries, up to 30 % with the best cannon; wax on the bus that is hit takes some of that off, never under 5 %. A ram shakes by its closing speed (RAMPAGE.md's bump, smash, wreck and head-on: 5, 15, 30 and 50 %), and a head-on shakes both. |
| The chance to run | The bus that was hit spins, is flung clear in a forced gear (built already) and can't be hit again for 3 s. |
| The spill | The coins hang as a ring for 5 s. Whoever flies through them has them: the hitter first, a passer-by, or the bus that lost them. |
| Clean | "Alive" is "clean": the time since a bus was last hit. Each clean minute adds a quarter to what paint pays, up to 3 times. A clean bus shines, and at the top it is gold. A hit resets it. |
| The bounty | Hitting a shining bus pays 10 coins for each of its clean minutes, from the city, on top of the spill. So a long life is worth hunting even when the bus has just banked. |
| Size | A bus grows with the coins it carries: more to win, slower to turn, easier to see and to hit. |
| Schools | Every real school, from the map's own data, is a bank and a safe place. Within 60 m of one every bus drops to School Zone (the gear exists), nothing can be hit, nothing is earned and no clock runs. Hover 2 s and the coins are banked: "BANKED 640 AT MISSION HIGH". A player picks a home school. |
| The wilds | No buildings, so nothing to earn, and the clean clock stands still. The ocean, the headlands and one day Antarctica are places to hide, not to live. |
| Heat | A neighbourhood's crews are as tough as it is rich: more buses, better cannons, more wax, from the same measured worth. Quiet streets for a new player, downtown for a strong one, in any city with no hand-made list. |
| The bell | Every 5 minutes the bell rings and the richest bus in the city is IT for 2 minutes: gold, and pointed out to every bus. If IT stays clean, what it carries doubles. Not Recess: nothing is fenced and nobody is pulled in. |
| Upgrades | Bought with banked coins, never with money: the cannon (shakes out more), wax (loses less), the tank (more paint), the magnet (takes a spill from farther off). |

**"Why not camp in Antarctica?"** Nothing pays there and the clock stands still. **"Is the big city for pros?"** Yes: it pays most and its crews hit hardest. **"What does painting give?"** The steady money, and the city in your colours. **"What does hunting give?"** The fast money: the spill and the bounty. **"A long lifespan?"** A clean bus earns up to three times as much, and carries a bounty for it.

## A trip

Leave school with an empty bus. Paint the nearest blocks. A rival crew's bus comes past, fat with coins: hit it, fly through its ring. Now this bus is the fat one, and shining. Downtown pays three times as much. The bell rings. One more block, or run for school? Four minutes later it is banked or it is somebody else's. Then again.

## Why it would be hard to put down

- **Seconds:** a hit bursts a bus open like a piñata, and nobody knows how much was in it until it bursts.
- **Minutes:** greed against fear. The stake is on screen as the bus's own size and shine.
- **A trip (about 5 minutes):** out, up, home. A trip that ends two blocks from school with 900 coins lost is a story a child tells.
- **A day:** the next upgrade is always close, and the first look in the Garage closer.
- **A season:** a new real city.
- **A friend:** a dare by link.

**Against Fortnite, plainly.**

| Fortnite | Here |
|---|---|
| A match with a beginning, a squeeze and an ending | A trip out and home, and the bell every 5 minutes |
| Loot | Coins from paint; a fat bus is the chest |
| Seasons that change the map | Seasons that are real cities. San Francisco first; London and Amsterdam are baked; the Moon is a season of its own |
| Skins and dances | COINS.md's Garage: liveries, a roof pet, the Dragon Dance; horns and a victory wiggle |
| Squads of friends, live | By link first (below). Live rooms need a server. This is where most of Fortnite's pull is, and we don't have it on day one |
| Free | Free |

**Not taken:** loot boxes and any random draw, power for money, timed sales, login streaks, a number that nags. The players are children. Epic agreed to pay 520 million dollars in 2022, in a settlement with the US Federal Trade Commission, over children's privacy and over tricking players into purchases.

## Other people

1. **Now, no server:** every other bus is the computer's, flying as a crew that earns, carries, banks, hunts the fat and runs when it is the fat one. They are crews, never dressed up as people.
2. **By link, no server:** a dare. "14 minutes clean in the Mission. Come and get me." The link carries the sender's look, upgrades and record (a new field in `docs/specs/core/LINK.md`'s card, whose format is versioned), and lands the friend in that place against the sender's bus, flown by the computer.
3. **Later, with a server and the lawyer's answer:** rooms of a few dozen real buses in one city, friends joining by link. Then school against school: every coin banked counts for a real school on the city's board, with no player named.

## Seasons: the world is the content

Each season is a real city, a new cannon, a new bell game (tag first; then hide and seek, king of the hill on a landmark, the gold bus that crosses the city) and a shelf of looks. The world render run becomes the content plan, and yesterday's scan rebuild (`exp/ladies`, `docs/specs/sf/LADIES.md`) is how a season's city comes out looking like itself. From orbit (the climb is built) the grey Earth could show what a player has painted. The Moon stays the secret at the top.

## Money

As answered the same day: nothing that paints, speeds or protects is ever sold (STUDIO.md part 10's "never" stands). Coins are earned, never sold. Money comes from looks and a season's pass of looks, and first from children's web-game sites that share their ads, which want school laptops and Android as well as iPhones. The printed postcard stays.

- Rival crews' buses about their own business, the shell, the wave, the ram by closing speed and the head-on (`docs/specs/factions/RAMPAGE.md` sections 2, 3 and 11).
- Being hit: the colour flood, the barrel roll, the fling in a forced gear. That is the chance to run.
- The squares, their stages, the Lift and a takeover (`src/factions/cityAreas.ts`, `docs/specs/factions/OPENWORLD.md`).
- A square's worth and the ledger, on paper (`docs/specs/core/COINS.md`).
- Crews, and the bus wearing its crew.
- The link, the save and the safe save (`docs/specs/core/LINK.md`, `STORE.md`), the Flyby (`FLYBY.md`).
- School Zone, the gear. Orbit, the Moon. Three cities.

## What is new, for a test

Coins carried and banked, with the ledger. The shake, the ring, the 3 s. Clean, shine and the bounty. A small index of schools from the map's data, and their safe circles. The crews' own purse: earning, carrying, banking, hunting, running. A bare Garage with the four upgrades. The bell and IT. On screen: what the bus carries, and its shine.

## The test

Inside San Francisco, against the computer's crews: downtown is the big city, the Sunset is the small town, the ocean is Antarctica. On an experiment branch, as `exp/ladies` was. Played by the first player again, five or more other children and a few adults. What decides: do they play ten minutes with nobody asking, do they ask for it the next day, do they understand "coins on the bus can be taken, coins at school are safe" with no words from us, and what do they buy first.

## What it would change

1. **STUDIO.md's spine.** The postcard stops being the spine and becomes the brag and the dare.
2. **STUDIO.md part 6:** "the coins and store plan" comes back.
3. **COINS.md:** "a hit never takes coins from the one hit" and "looks and places, never power". Here a hit takes a share, and coins buy four kinds of power. Nothing is sold for money either way.
4. **"Stop ... new rival or life features"** (2026-11-14): the crews need a purse and a reason to hunt.
5. **A hit on a bus** (SHOTS.md, FACTIONS.md's Possessed) also shakes coins out.
6. **"No localized matches":** the bell is city-wide and fenced nowhere. The producer's call whether that is still a match.
7. **"No number while flying"** (COINS.md): a player must know the stake. My pick: the bus's size and shine, and a small count by it.
8. **Who it is for** (STUDIO.md part 2, "We market to adults"): children first, or both. COINS.md's five questions for the lawyer stand, with one more: a child's home school must never leave the phone.
9. **"iPhone Safari first":** the web-game sites want school laptops and Android. Later, and unmeasured.

Unchanged: paint is the only saturated thing; no new button on the play screen; no server for the test; the gods on hold.

## What could sink it

- The computer's crews feel like robots. They must fly, hunt and run like children do.
- Flying is harder than driving. The first player managed; five more will say.
- It has run on one iPhone 17 Pro and machines with no GPU. A school laptop is not that.
- Taking coins from a real child is not taking them from a bot. Rooms need the caps and the grace COINS.md's critics asked for, and new players must be worth nothing to hit.
- One player so far. That is why the test comes before the plan is rewritten.
- "As addictive as Fortnite" is not a thing anyone can promise. Fortnite's hold is friends playing live. We get there, if the test earns it, in three steps: the computer's crews, links, rooms.

## Not chosen

- **Matches of a hundred, last bus flying.** Needs a server on day one, and most players lose at once.
- **One life, lost whole.** The producer's rule is kinder and makes more fights: a bus that is hit is back in it in 3 s.
- **Turf war only.** Paint with nobody to fight is what the first player flew past.
