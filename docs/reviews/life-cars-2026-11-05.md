# Life's cars, boats and picnics (feat/life, 1cad05e to ff8ba06)

The first account, 2026-11-05, for the second account. Life's code only: `src/life/` (`sim/cars.ts` and the heavy pool, `sim/boats.ts`, `sim/parks.ts`, `kinds.ts`, `Life.ts` and its aim, `lifeWorker.ts`, `pools.ts`, `paint.ts`, `lifeBoot.ts`), life's lines in `src/main.ts`, and `tools/life/`. Read at 92b4884, which past ff8ba06 only touches `CLAUDE.md`. Every file:line below is at 92b4884.

**Verdict.** The sims are well made where it counts most for a shared world: no randomness or wall clock in them, cars keep to one-way streets and to the right decks on both bridges, the cars in reach don't change with which regions are loaded, every sailboat loop stays on open water, grey stays grey, and people are in the parks. But it isn't ready for main. One blocker: the new aim cone hands the shot to a car or bus in the street when the player aims at a building, and along the nose it hands it to a car up to 1.7 km down the road. That is the reverse of what the producer asked for, and painting buildings is the game. Seven majors follow: ferries drawn inside each other at their berths (the Ferry Building is the first place the report sends the producer), one vehicle in seven drawn inside another, the ferry key breaking the signed contract, the picnic cap filling the lawns behind the bus first, life's triangles several times its budget by the code's own count (L3 still unbuilt), wakes that are straight bars instead of staying on the water, and no buses on Market Street. Nothing ran on a GPU: the laptop never came free.

## How it ran

- Three readers, each read the range and ran small Node experiments on San Francisco's real files (the 270 `g_*.bin`, `routes.bin`, and for the parks the `r_*.bin` tiles) through life's own modules, imported from the worktree at 92b4884. Each reader's findings went to a verifier who re-read the code and re-ran the main numbers with scripts of their own. Only what a verifier held is below, at the verifier's severity.
- The scripts and their outputs stay on the laptop in `sb-checks/life-cars-2026-11-05/` (and its `refute2/`, `verify/`, `verify2/` and `tests-review/` folders), not in the repo. Every run stayed under 165 MB.
- **No builds, no browser, no GPU.** The GPU checks waited 110 minutes on a gate (no bake running, over 1,000 MB free on two polls in a row) and never passed: first a bake and pack, then another job's gate, then that job's `GPU.lock` until the end. Free memory read 167 to 1,573 MB. So `tsc`, `vite`, `iosfloor`, smoke, paint, sky, flag-off, warmcheck and any flight weren't run, and no frame was looked at. `determinism.mjs` and `alloc.mjs` weren't run either, except one reader's run of `alloc.mjs`.
- Timings are this laptop's V8, often with another job on the CPU. They are not an A15's JavaScriptCore.

## Findings

### Blocker

**B1. The aim gives the shot to a vehicle in the street when the player aims at a building, and along the nose to a car far down the road.**
- *What a player sees.* Aiming at a house beside a busy street, the glint jumps to a car or bus in the street and the tap paints it. Flying low and level along a long street, the glint leaves the buildings in front and sits on a car 3-5 px tall up to 1.7 km away, and the breath goes there. At 3:30 am, with nine cars on a 4.3 km street, a car still glints three frames in four.
- *Where.* `src/game/Aim.ts:322` (`near()`: an onLine mover wins whenever no building is latched, with no comparison against the building), `:325-331` (the second call along the nose, `moverReach` 2,500 m, `config.ts:531`; its comment says it's for planes). `src/life/Life.ts:745` (an onLine mover may stand up to L + 60 m along the ray), `:753` (the cone, now 1 m + 0.6 x size + 4.5 % of the distance, was 0.5 x size + 2 %), `:59` (`GROUND_CONE` 0.045). `kinds.ts:46-48` makes picnics, cars and trucks onLine.
- *Evidence.* The aim rows in the table. With 1cad05e's cone the aim ray took 0.0 % at every height in the level-flight run; the nose call took 16.5-69.8 %, so the nose path is older, but cars only arrived in this range and ff8ba06 roughly doubled it at 25-45 m. No test bounds the cone: `paint.mjs:211` and `alloc.mjs:205` pass for any radius from 5 to 30 m. The runs have no real buildings, so `clearTo` didn't run; on a straight street the chord stays over the road, so few would be stopped (inferred).
- *Severity.* The tests verifier held both parts as a blocker. The runtime verifier held the nose ray as major and the street balance as minor: it reads the contract's "a mover nearer the line of fire than the building target wins" (`CONTRACT.md:234`) as nearer the bus, which is how `Aim.ts:321` and `kinds.ts:37-41` read it, and under that reading a bus in front of a house may win. Either way, at 15-25 m up a vehicle takes every shot along a busy arterial, and the producer asked to hit cars, not to stop hitting buildings. I rank it a blocker for the merge.
- *Fix.* Don't take onLine kinds on the nose's ray (drop an onLine answer from the second call, or pass `Life.aim` a flag), and cap an onLine mover at the aim ray's own length + `ON_LINE_PAST`. Size the cone in screen pixels (the vehicle's box plus a few px), not in % of distance. Let a building the line lands on keep the shot unless the vehicle is clearly nearer the line. Add paint rows: a roof beside a busy arterial at 09:30, aimed at from 15 m, 45 m and 150 m along the street, keeps the glint; and a row bounding the cone in pixels. Then one flight by the producer to tune the feel.
- The same nose path takes a non-onLine mover over open water up to 1.25 x 2,500 m ahead (`Life.ts:745`), so a ferry 3 km off can glint while the bus skims the bay. Read only.

### Major

**M1. Ferries are drawn inside one another at their berths, overnight, and on shared first legs.**
- *What a player sees.* At the Ferry Building, Oyster Point, Larkspur, Alcatraz and Vallejo, two to four ferries sit in one place with their hulls through each other. At 13:00 local, 25 pairs are at rest 2.8-14.3 m apart at shared slips. At 06:20 the two Oyster Point lines sail out 25.7 m apart at 15 m/s, one 40 m ferry through the other.
- *Where.* `src/life/sim/boats.ts:47-48` (vessels = ceil(cycle / head), and each vessel's round trip is shorter than vessels x head), `:86-114` (vessel j's day starts at 06:00 + j x head and it lies at s = 0 before and after), `:127-137` (every ferry at rest sits at s = 0 or L, its ±60 m chord clamped, so 25-30 m from the line's end). No berth per vessel, no spacing between lines sharing a slip.
- *Evidence.* The ferry rows in the table, from two separate scripts. The overnight pairs at exactly 0.0 m (Treasure Island, Larkspur, Alcatraz, Vallejo) draw as one hull. The four within 4.2 m at Oyster Point are the "two ferries tied up" whose hand-over jump `LIFE.md:43` reports.
- *Severity.* Blocker by the reader, major by the verifier: it breaks no rule or contract and the fix is small, but it is the showcase scene.
- *Fix.* Give each vessel its own berth spot (offset along the slip by index, or lay vessel j > 0 up at the far terminal). Make each round trip exactly vessels x head, the slack taken as dwell. Stagger each line's first sailing by a hash of its key. Keep two lines' arrivals at one slip apart. Add a determinism row: no two ferry hulls overlap at any minute of a day.

**M2. About one vehicle in seven is drawn inside another, nose to tail in one lane.**
- *What a player sees.* Low over the streets and on both bridges, cars, buses and trucks merged into each other, two bodies in one place. It undercuts the larger size meant to make them easy to see.
- *Where.* `src/life/sim/cars.ts:352-357`: the only spacing is a way's slot k against slot k-1. Nothing spaces it against cars arriving from other ways, and there's no following. `:143-229`: every driven way, each split piece included, is its own birth stream. `:405` puts every bus and truck in the kerb lane.
- *Evidence.* 15.1 % and 15.5 % at FiDi from two scripts, 8.9 % on the Bay Bridge, 12.3 % on the Golden Gate. Of 218 same-way, same-lane pairs at FiDi, none were both born on that way (the k-1 check works); in 52 one was born on top of an arrival, in 166 both came in from other ways.
- *Fix.* Birth cars only at a road's real starts (junctions of three or more roads, dead ends, service and driveway sources), with the rate scaled by the length of the chain of splits after them. Add a deterministic de-overlap pass in the worker: sort the kept set by (way, lane, distance along) and hold each follower its length plus a gap behind its leader. Add an overlap count to `determinism.mjs`.

**M3. The ferry key and its paint's `until` break the signed contract.**
- *What a player sees.* A ferry painted at 21:00 stays painted through the whole next day, then turns grey 24 h after the hit, possibly mid-crossing.
- *Where.* `boats.ts:142` (`ferry:${f.key}:${j}`, no service day), `:157` (UNTIL = 86400); `Life.ts:812-818` turns it into the snapshot's time + 24 h; `Life.ts:299` stores every key but `picnic:` and `car:`. `CONTRACT.md` 0.10 section 3: `ferry:<route key>:<vessel>:<service day>` (`:210`), and in the Agreed "What's saved", kept to "a ferry's end of its service day" (`:240`). No `CONTRACT:` commit in the range (`git log 1cad05e..92b4884 -- docs/specs/earth/CONTRACT.md` prints nothing).
- *Also.* The sailboat key is `sail:${h.key}:${day}:${b}` (`boats.ts:265`): day before boat, a day count since 1970, where the contract has `sail:<marina key>:<boat>:<day>`. The contract's key table still lists `walk:` and has no `picnic:`.
- *Severity.* Major by the sims verifier (a persisted key is costly to change once records exist); the runtime and tests verifiers held the same thing as minor.
- *Fix.* Key ferries by the local service day and set UNTIL to the end of that day's last crossing, or propose the code's keys in a `CONTRACT:` commit with `picnic:` added and `walk:` removed. Add paint rows: a ferry still painted after a reload, and a record kept at until - 1 s and gone at until + 1 s (L5's case, which 1c3998e's clock change most needs).

**M4. The 1,200-picnic cap fills lawns in the order they were walked, so the grass under and ahead of the bus comes up emptiest.**
- *What a player sees.* Flying along Golden Gate Park on a weekend afternoon, the meadow ahead is half empty or bare while lawns behind keep their blankets. Groups freed by the cap pop in at full size. Two players at one spot see different picnics depending on where they came from.
- *Where.* `src/life/lifeWorker.ts:51` (`PICNICS_MOST` 1200), `:247-254` (picnics filled over `lawns`, a Map in insertion order, against `n0 + PICNICS_MOST`); the order is the host's walk (`Life.ts:196-258`, tiles kept to 1.6 km). The comment at `:49-51` says nearest first, true only when each tile arrived.
- *Evidence.* The picnic row in the table: 35 % filled at 0-300 m against 75 % at 700-1,000 m; 0 of 1,200 ahead on the test park. Both verifiers held it from the code; neither re-ran the park numbers.
- *Fix.* Each tick, fill the lawns nearest the bus first (a reused array of at most about 60 tiles, sorted by distance). Near the cap, thin by a hash share so groups grow in and out.

**M5. Life's triangles are several times its budget by the code's own count, it draws six times against five, and L3 still doesn't exist.**
- *What a player sees.* Nothing measured. By the code, likely frame drops on an A15 over a park next to downtown on a weekend afternoon, worst after a MEGA paints the picnics and they all dance.
- *Where.* `src/life/pools.ts:13` (4,096 / 2,048 / 512 / 512), `:193-199` (every instance runs every box of its pool, other kinds folded to a point), `:403` and the other pools (never frustum-culled), `:460-471` (four pools, specks and marks: six draws). `Life.ts:500-508` (up to four dancers a painted picnic in the small pool), `:432-433` (a full pool skips later rows with no speck, so grey groups can vanish). `lifeWorker.ts:51`, `parks.ts:33` (density 0.016, 300 a tile).
- *Evidence.* The triangle row in the table. `LIFE.md:39` counts 330 K for 1,200 picnics and about 120 K for rush-hour vehicles. The contract gives all of life 80 K scene triangles and 1.5 ms of main thread (`CONTRACT.md:178`, `:180`), and the street alone measured 1.17 M at Alamo Square (`:186`); `CLAUDE.md` caps the frame at 1.0-1.5 M. A full small pool is 1.13 M triangles in one draw. O1 is Agreed at 5 draws (`:373`) and the contract has no heavy pool. `LIFE.md:104` still says L3 "waits for the boats"; the boats are in. The earlier GPU review's m2 is still open.
- *Fix.* Split the small pool so a blanket instance doesn't carry the people's boxes and a dancer doesn't carry the blanket's; draw dancers only for the painted groups nearest the lens; give picnics and trucks a smaller model under a few pixels; cull instances outside the view before upload; cap picnics by pixels and distance, not count. Build L3 and run it at Dolores and Golden Gate Park on a Saturday, grey and painted, and at FiDi at rush hour. Propose the sixth draw and the heavy pool in `CONTRACT.md` with those numbers.

**M6. A painted boat's wake is a straight bar that moves with the boat, not a wake along its route.**
- *What a player sees.* Behind a painted ferry, a straight rainbow bar 280-600 m long that swings with the ferry at a bend or into a berth and travels with it. Behind a painted sailboat on its loop, a straight 110-170 m bar sticking out from the loop's tight ends and pivoting round.
- *Where.* `Life.ts:357-363` (every point laid straight back along the current velocity, `px = x - ux * back`, for speed x min(40 s, age)), `:462-464` (every wake uses it). Cars' tracks, by contrast, follow where the car has been (`:566-640`).
- *Evidence.* Read from the code, not seen on a GPU. The Agreed O6 says "a ferry's wake is its route from where it was painted to where it is now" (`CONTRACT.md:378`); the approved plan says a wake "that stays on the water along the route" (`LIVING.md:23`, `:42`). Sailboat loop ends turn on radii of about 15-280 m (`boats.ts:218-219`).
- *Fix.* Lay wakes the way tracks are laid, from points kept behind each painted boat. For a ferry, rebuild the route's polyline from the moment it was painted to now (the route is a function of the clock), fading by age.

**M7. Market Street's car-free blocks get no buses.**
- *What a player sees.* Flying up Market Street from the Ferry Building at rush hour: the city's main bus street with no bus on it.
- *Where.* `cars.ts:147`, `:159` leave out every UNDRAWN or NO_CARS edge, buses included. `src/world/lifeData.ts:44` sets NO_CARS where cars are denied even when buses and taxis are allowed. `GRAPHS.md:28`: 383 such segments, "52 are Market Street's, buses allowed". `determinism.mjs:194-216` checks only the bus share on primary and secondary roads, so it passes with Market empty.
- *Evidence.* From the code and `GRAPHS.md`; not flown, Market's edges not counted here.
- *Fix.* This is the contract change behind bus routes, which the first account has taken on as C14 (`docs/TODO.md`, ac2f436). First, one bus-allowed bit per road edge from OSM's bus and psv access tags, so only buses drive those NO_CARS edges. Then route ids from OSM's `route=bus` relations, for real Muni lines. Then a determinism row: buses on Market's car-free blocks at 09:30.

### Minor

**m1. Most cars are born mid-road, so several a second grow out of or shrink into the street in plain view.** At FiDi rush hour, 3.5-4.5 appear and 3.5 vanish each second within 550-600 m of the bus; 63 % of births are at a mere split in one road (`cars.ts:143-202`, `:346-351`, grow over 1.2 s at `:408`, life ends wherever it is at `:358-360`). Major by the reader, minor by the verifier: the lifetime is the second account's declared call. Fix with M2's births at real starts; end a life at a corner or a sink; lengthen lives to 2-4 minutes with a per-car path cache in the worker (still a pure function).

**m2. The cars tick walks four times the cars it keeps; its first distance cull culls nothing.** `cars.ts:344` (`d0 - vmax * LIFE_MAX > reach`, 2.2 km against 1.2 km), every slot placed before the distance test at `:406-407`, a template-string key and FNV-1a for every kept car every tick at `:409`. About 5 ms at 08:00 FiDi on an idle-ish laptop against the contract's 6 ms for the whole worker tick. Major by the reader, minor by the verifier (off the main thread, near the budget, not clearly over). Fix: bound each birth way by travel time to the reach (a reverse search, redone each 100 m), skip slots that can't reach it, and hash the key from integers.

**m3. Sailboats heel into the wind, sail dead upwind once a lap, and speed up and slow down round the loop.** `boats.ts:277` (BANK = HEEL x sin(heading - WIND); with `Life.ts:485`'s 'YZX' Euler the mast always leans upwind, derived independently by the verifier), `:258` (angle advances at v / ((A + B) / 2)), `:216-234` (an ellipse covers every heading). Fix: negate the bank; parametrise by arc length; lay each loop as a reach across the wind with a tack at one end and a gybe at the other, nothing within 45° of the wind.

**m4. Buses and trucks wander into alleys, and the street classes' contrast washes out.** The type comes only from the birth road (`cars.ts:353`); turns weigh angle alone (`:218-222`). Buses are 8.6 % of vehicles on service ways against a 1 % birth share; secondary to service traffic is 4.3:1 against a target of 13:1. Fix: weight `next()` by class (stay on the same class or bigger), keep heavies off service and living ways unless it's the only way on.

**m5. Every freeway exit takes a third or more of the traffic.** No weight of their own for LINK or ROUNDABOUT (`cars.ts:218-222`). Median 32 % at 7 diverges round FiDi, 41 % at 9 near the Golden Gate. Fix: weight a link at a diverge to about 10 %.

**m6. Cars keep weekday rush hours on weekends.** One HOURS profile (`cars.ts:32`, `:325-328`); parks and boats already read the weekday. Fix: a later, lower weekend profile from the local day at birth.

**m7. Picnics and sailboats stay out after dark.** `parks.ts:36` (home at 19:30 every day), `boats.ts:179` (sailing to 19:00). Dolores has 76 blankets at 18:00 on a November Saturday; 8 boats are out at 17:30. Sunset about 17:00 is the reviewers' figure, not a run. Fix: end the day at local sunset plus about 30 minutes, from the sky's own sun maths.

**m8. The ferry timetable runs pairs in convoy, and event-only lines sail every day.** Vessel j repeats every cycle, shorter than vessels x head (`boats.ts:47-48`, `:86-92`): Alcatraz leaves 06:00, 06:30, 06:32, 07:02, 07:04. All 19 lines leave at exactly 06:00. `ferryLines` reads no seasonal flag, but `routes.bin` sets none either (0 of 20, though three are named "(Seasonal)"): that part is the world's. Fix with M1; honour weekday and event lines once the world flags them.

**m9. Mover keys are never forgotten, on either thread.** `lifeWorker.ts:71` (`sent`, cleared only at init, `:283`), `Life.ts:84`, `:167-172` (never pruned); car keys carry a slot count (`cars.ts:409`), so every car is new. About 25,000-53,000 keys an hour downtown, a few MB an hour (estimated). With 32-bit hashes the chance of a collision by 53,000 keys is about 28 %; a colliding key is never sent (`lifeWorker.ts:237`), so two cars share one paint. Fix: drop keys not seen for a while (except painted ones) and resend when they come back; add an alloc row with new keys every snapshot for 10 minutes.

**m10. Cars blink at short underpasses and pop in at the edge of the reach.** `cars.ts:376` skips a car on a TUNNEL way whole, with no fade (188 drivable tunnel edges under 40 m, median 14 m); `:407`'s 700 m-1.2 km thinning has no fade. Fix: fade by distance to a tunnel mouth; scale a fringe car in over about 50 m past its threshold.

**m11. A failed worker tick freezes life, and frozen movers slide off along their velocity.** `lifeWorker.ts:229-275` has no try/finally, so a throw keeps its buffer and the host never ticks again (`Life.ts:80`, `:306`); the carry-on `dt` is uncapped (`Life.ts:410`, `:415`); no `worker.onerror` in `src/life`. The throw is hypothetical; no code was found that obviously throws. Fix: post the buffer back in a finally, add `onerror`, and hold or fade movers past about 0.5 s.

**m12. Kept paint writes one transaction per landed hit, with no journal.** `paint.ts:63-84` per record, from `land()` inside the frame (`Life.ts:299`); no pagehide or visibilitychange listener in `src/life`, where O14 and section 3 (`CONTRACT.md:386`, `:248`) ask for GeoPaint's way. Past the cap, `put` also sorts all 2,000 records each time (`paint.ts:68-75`). Fix: queue and write a few a frame in one transaction, with a journal on hide.

**m13. The tests check less than their names in five places.**
- The ferry wake row reads ribbon 0 without checking whose it is (`paint.mjs:247-251`), and a painted car's tracks earlier in the same page would pass it; the contrail row went from `=== 1` to `>= 1` marks.
- L1's WebKit leg runs scripted planes only (`tools/life/vectors.mjs:8`, `:17`, `src/life/check.ts:2`), so cars, boats, picnics and `zoneOffset`'s Intl are never compared across engines, while the phone's page reads "MATCHES ... WITHIN 1 CM".
- The hand-over limit was loosened twice (78c7fc4 worst < 4 and median < 1.5 px; 868616d worst < 6 and median < 3; ff8ba06 measured past the street's own extrapolation, `sky.mjs:105-106`, and only `plane:` keys can fail, `:113-116`, unknown keys exempt).
- Smoke's night row passes on one lit ferry speck (`smoke.mjs:108`, `Life.ts:449-456`); "near ones as models" is never compared with the models (`:83`); DECK rows are counted but never height-checked (`:70`).
- No row checks grey until painted (an unpainted instance's ramp against the grey set) or the movers' sizes (`LOOK_SCALE`, `LOOK_LEN`, `PICNIC_SCALE`).

Fixes: record each ribbon's mover hash in qa; add vectors for each sim; keep a raw plane bound and fail on unknown keys; tighten the three smoke rows; add the grey and size rows.

### Nit

- **n1. A jump in traffic at the clock change.** Births up to 90 s back use the current minute's offset (`cars.ts:351`, `:356`): at 01:00 on the night the clocks go back, 17 cars appear within 700 m in a tenth of a second. Look up the offset at the birth time.

## The second account's calls

- **A car lives a minute or so, growing in where it starts and out where it ends.** Keep the closed form: deterministic, cheap, jump-proof. As built it reads as magic: 3.5-4.5 cars a second appear and vanish within 600 m at rush hour, mostly at a split in one road, and those streams pile into one another (M2, m1). Birth at real starts, end at corners or sinks, stretch lives to 2-4 minutes with a path cache, add the follow pass. A painted car also ends with its slot, on average about 34 s after the hit, in front of a hovering bus: let the host pass painted hashes in the tick so a painted car drives on while it's near (painted movers are already outside the determinism rule). And prune the keys (m9).
- **Traffic thins from 700 m to 1.2 km.** Right at street level: a hard edge would show a disc, and thinning by each car's own hash is stable. Fade the fringe in (m10). From 500 m to 2.5 km up, 1.2 km is inside the view with cars still 3-6 px, so a disc follows the bus: tie the reach to pixel size as the bus climbs (`LIVING.md` rule 4).
- **The ferries' timetable.** A good educated guess in shape: 06:00 to 22:00, half-hourly or hourly, 7, 12 and 15 m/s, 8-minute dwells. Not a timetable yet: vessels in convoy, every line at exactly 06:00, vessels and lines sharing one spot, game-day lines daily (M1, m8). Key by service day as the contract says (M3) and lay the wake on the route (M6). It's the showcase scene, so M1 comes first.
- **Sailboats sailing loops off their marina.** A fine first pass: deterministic, cheap, every loop on open water. A sailor would spot the heel, the upwind leg and the speed (m3), and players will spot the straight wake on the tight ends (M6). Laying loops as reaches sets up the painted regatta as a real windward-leeward course.
- **Not on the list, and the one I'd change first: the aim cone (B1).**
- **Dolores.** It peaks at a blanket every 13.6 m, about 550 people on 3.9 ha of park grass, the same density as every park. The producer called Dolores's peak "wildly chaotic"; a denser data-driven rule for small central parks is worth putting to them. Judged by numbers only: the producer's photo wasn't read.
- **Bus routes.** Taken by the first account as C14 (above, M7). Life's side is small: a bus-allowed bit and route ids per edge, buses born on route edges and kept to them at junctions. A 60 ft articulated look would read as Muni.

## The open item: the ferries' 7.8 px jump at the 15 km hand-over

Not found. Life's own two placements account for 0.39 px: a ferry 12.7 km off with the bus 15 km up sits 12.7 m lower on the ellipsoid than on the flat map, 8.2 m across the line of sight (0.25 px on the vista lens). So it isn't life's geometry. Two leads in the flight's code, neither run:
1. `TripView` eases the lens from the street's turbo-widened hfov toward 38° from the flight's first frame (`TripView.ts:169-170`, `LENS_RATE` 1.2/s), and the street's hfov carries Turbo's widening (`CameraRig.ts:210`, `Turbo.ts:117-119`), up to 6.9° in Turbo 3: about 0.7 % radial zoom a frame, roughly 3 px at the frame's edge, so not all of it.

To tell them apart, have `sky.mjs` print `camera.fov` and the camera's world quaternion on frames k-3 to k, and split each mover's miss into radial and tangential parts about the screen's centre: radial and growing from the centre points to the lens, uniform to a rotation, shrinking with the mover's distance to the camera's position.
