# feat/above 4f33648 to dac73f6: a review stopped part-way

The second account, 2026-11-14. This review was stopped by the producer's direction of the same day (reviews of `feat/above` and `feat/handling` stop; the second account reviews release candidates only). It is **not a review and gives no verdict**. It is what its readers had found when it stopped, kept because the work was already done and `feat/above` still merges into the core and the one link.

How far it got: three readers (the masks' life and uploads; the draw; the checks and the merges) each finished and wrote findings. A second reader attacked the first and the third lists; the draw's list was not attacked. Where a second reader's verdict is given, the finding was reproduced by it. Nothing below was checked by me, but one thing I ran myself before the readers reported it, and it is the one to act on:

**The branch breaks seven Node rows that pass on its base.** `GroundPaint.flush` now calls `renderer.initTexture` (`src/world/GroundPaint.ts:1147`), which the tools' stand-in renderers lack (`tools/feel-sim/lib.ts:14`, `s_boom.ts:38`, `s_falls.ts:29`). On dac73f6 `s_lob`, `s_bomb`, `s_boom`, `s_falls`, `s_aim`, `s_alt` and controls-sim's `s_shots` each end "TypeError: renderer.initTexture is not a function at GroundPaint.flush"; on 4f33648 they run to their end ("lob checks: all 1231 passed" and so on). ABOVE-BUILT.md's table of 64 rows names none of them, as run or as not run. These are the rows that hold the Lob's latch and the resting thumb, so they must be green again before this branch meets the core. My own runs on dac73f6: `tsc` 0; build 0; "iOS 15.0 parses every chunk"; "s_rmask: all passed"; "farchunk: every box the same in chunks of 4 and 8", with `--ref` against 4f33648's `farBlocks.ts` the same; `tools/moon/paint-check.mjs` "all pass"; `s_lob.ts`, `s_boom.ts`, `s_falls.ts`, `s_bomb.ts`, `s_aim.ts` the TypeError.

Numbers start at M200, m200 and n200. M200 and m201 are the fault above, seen by two readers; n200 and n203 are one nit seen twice.


## Major

**M200. Five of the shots' Node rows throw on this branch; made able to run, the Bomb's and the Boom's go red on what 1a changed.** `src/world/GroundPaint.ts:1147`
- *What happens.* On the branch head, run the rows the shots are gated by: `node tools/feel-sim/run.mjs s_bomb.ts`, `s_boom.ts`, `s_falls.ts`, `s_lob.ts`, and `node tools/controls-sim/run.mjs s_shots.ts`. Each dies with `TypeError: renderer.initTexture is not a function` the first time paint reaches a coarse region's mask and it is flushed. On 4f33648's source the same rows run to their verdicts. So on this branch, and on whatever it merges into, nothing checks the Bomb, the Sonic Boom, the Waterfall, the Lob or the Comet in Node, and none of the five is among the 64 rows of ABOVE-BUILT.md's table. No player meets this; it is the suite that is broken.
- *Why.* `if (!props.__webglTexture) renderer.initTexture(r.tex!);` and uploadRectFrom after it (upload.ts:36, "nothing here falls back"), where 4f33648's uploadRect returned false on a renderer that holds no GL texture. The mask is on by default, and the harness's stand-in renderers have no initTexture: tools/feel-sim/lib.ts:14 `fakeRenderer = { properties: { get: () => ({}) }, getContext: () => ({}), state: {} }`, and `counting` in s_falls.ts:29 and s_boom.ts:38. Given one (my copy), two rows go red on 1a itself. (a) s_bomb.ts:205 `ok(ups <= 1, ... at most one paint upload a texture a frame)`, the rule upload.ts:5-6 still states ("paint uploads must stay at most one call per texture per frame"): a region's mask takes one call a dirty slot, so several into one texture. (b) s_boom's flights reset with `city.ground.resetAll()` (s_boom.ts:53), which clears what is saved and leaves loaded masks. At 4f33648 a mask ended at the next change of detail and its paint went with it; with a mask's life kept apart from its views it stays, and a flight's hole reads the earlier flights' rings. In the game resetAll is followed by location.reload() (main.ts:1161-1167), so (b) is the tools' alone.
- *Evidence.* Ran, in my own worktree copy of dac73f6. Head as it is: s_bomb, s_boom, s_falls, s_lob and s_shots each exit 1 on `TypeError: renderer.initTexture is not a function ... at GroundPaint.flush` (s_bomb after 16 of its 46 place rows); s_super, s_miss, s_deck, s_curb and s_turbo pass. 4f33648's src under the same tools: s_falls "falls checks: all 202 passed (70 rows)", s_boom "boom checks: all 199 passed (61 rows)", s_lob "lob checks: all 1231 passed", s_bomb "FAILED 3 of 385" (three ms bars), s_shots "FAILED 4 of 363" (four Comet rows). Head with the three stand-ins given an initTexture: s_falls 202 passed, s_lob 1231 passed, s_shots the same four Comet rows as the base; s_boom "FAILED 3 of 199" with "western-addition 490 m at 60 fps: the ground painted in the band and not in the hole (1 of the band's samples, 0.54 of the hole's)", the same at 30 fps, and one ms bar; s_bomb "noe 600 m, Bomb III: at most one paint upload a texture a frame (2)" in 2 of 5 runs, the texture printed as "512 x 512". My own rows: 16 taps on one coarse region go up as 16 calls into its one texture in one frame (180,412 bytes), and a painted region coming back as 16 calls, 1,048,576 bytes; after resetAll() and 2 km away and back at 450 m, 13,059 painted texels are still 13,059 with the mask and 0 with ?rmask=0. The ms bars swing on this machine (load 10) and are not counted against the branch.
- *Fix.* Give the three stand-ins an initTexture (a few lines each; tools/above/s_rmask.mjs's has one). Restate the upload rule as a call a slot in upload.ts's head and in s_bomb's bar. Have the rows forget loaded paint (city.reloadAll() after resetAll(), or resetAll() also clears the masks it holds). Then run the five rows at the head and add them to the table.
