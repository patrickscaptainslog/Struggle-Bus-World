# The shots redesign, the first account's branch (feat/shots fd6d413)

The second account, 2026-11-08, for the first account. A code review of `feat/shots` at fd6d413, against `origin/main` (merge base 058217c): hold to aim (`Input.ts`, `Hold.ts`), the Hose, the Comet, the Bomb, School Zone and Curb's flight, and their wiring in `main.ts` and `Splat.ts`. It read the code against SHOTS.md (on `feat/earth`, 23951a4), BUILT.md, HOOKS.md and CURB.md, and held the hooks the second account builds on to what HOOKS.md says, word for word. Docs and tools were left alone. Lines are at fd6d413. Numbers start at B40, M80, m80 and n80.

**Verdict.** No blocker. In what was read, the gestures, the Hose, the Comet, the Bomb's tiers and jumps, School Zone and Curb's flight do what their specs say. `tsc`, the build and the Safari 15 parse all pass, and the hold sim passes all its checks. Reading the new code found no per-frame allocation and no program compiled in flight. Nothing a player can reach on this branch alone is broken. One major is in the seam the duel will stand on, so fix it before the second account builds the rival lock on it: a homing shot fired from inside its own white window can't hit anything that moves (M80). The minors are places where the hooks don't do quite what HOOKS.md says (m80 to m83), and a tap that does nothing, silently, while the Hose pours (m84).

## Major

**M80. A homing shot fired inside its white window can't hit anything that moves.**
- *What a player sees.* Nothing yet, since nothing on this branch fires one. Once the duel is built on `holds.fire`, with HOOKS.md's own numbers (420 m/s, white 0.7 s), a lock on a rival inside about 290 m always misses if the rival is moving at all, even flying straight at Cruise. Lift, and the blob flies to where the rival was and goes by: "dodged", with no dodge made. A captain inside about 270 m can never hit a moving player either. The same goes for a boat lock with a white window: SHOTS.md says boats can't dodge, but a ferry crossing at 12 m/s, shot at 500 m/s from 250 to 320 m, gets missed.
- *Evidence.* `fire()` points the first heading straight at where the target is now (`Hold.ts:441`), with no lead: `tv` starts at zero, and it only gets a sample once two frames have seen the target (`:492-495`). The first `stepShot` turns white as soon as distance / speed ≤ `white` (`:501-504`) and only steers while it isn't white (`:509`). Fired from closer than speed × white, the shot is white from its first frame, so it never leads and never turns. The probe, the real class, target crossing, `hitRadius` as listed:

  | Shot | Target crossing at | 150 m | 250 m | 290 m | 320 m | 500 m | 800 m |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 420 m/s, white 0.7, turn 2.2, r 7 | 0 m/s | hit | hit | hit | hit | hit | hit |
  | same | 30 m/s | dodged | dodged | dodged | dodged | hit | hit |
  | same | 100 m/s | dodged | dodged | dodged | dodged | hit | hit |
  | 380 m/s, white 0.7, turn 1.8, r 6 (a captain's) | 30 m/s | dodged | dodged | dodged | hit | hit | hit |
  | 500 m/s, white 0.7, turn 2, r 6 (a boat lock) | 12 m/s | hit | dodged | dodged | dodged | hit | hit |

  BUILT.md's own check fired from 420 m crossing at 20 m/s (BUILT.md:72), which is outside the window, and `s_hold.ts`'s boat row is 400 m out at 15 m/s, also outside. HOOKS.md promises "Before the white it leads the target ... In the white it flies straight, so a hard turn or a flick then beats it." Inside the window, flying straight beats it too.
- *Fix.* Lead from the launch. Either take one more `target()` sample before the white can start, steering the first frame at the led point with no turn limit, or give `HomingOpts` an optional `vel(out)` so the first heading leads. Or cap the white at a share of the first time to go, so even a close shot steers for a moment. Then add a probe row inside the window: a lock at 200 m on a target crossing at 30 m/s hits if the target holds its line, and a hard turn in the white still beats it.

## Minor

- **m80. HOOKS.md sends the second account to the wrong object.** It says "`window.__qa.holds` is the same object" (HOOKS.md:5) and "`window.__qa.hoseState()` says what it's doing" (:125). Both are on `window.__game` (`main.ts:2250`, `:2305`, `:2313`). `window.__qa` is the tools' settings object, which the game reads (`main.ts:1557`). `tools/warmcheck.mjs` already uses `__game`. *Fix:* say `window.__game` in HOOKS.md.
- **m81. A `target()` that throws on the first call isn't caught, and the belly is gone.** HOOKS.md:46 says every homing shot's `target` and callbacks are caught. `fire()` spends the belly (`Hold.ts:430`) and plays the breath (`:435`), and only then calls `o.target(tgt)` outside any `try` (`:438`). The probe: the error came out of `fire()`, `tryBarf` had been called, and `stats.errors` stayed 0. The bus heaves and empties its belly, and no blob leaves. *Fix:* call `target()` first, inside a `try`, and refuse the shot (with the cough, and nothing spent) if it throws.
- **m82. A target that's never there doesn't end as `lost`.** HOOKS.md:87: if `target()` says false, "the shot flies on a beat and ends as a miss (`lost`)". That only happens if it was seen before (`Hold.ts:545`). If `target()` is false from the start, the shot flies flat along the bus's heading (`:442`) until it hits something or `maxTime` runs out (`:550`). The probe: it ended as `time` after 8.02 s, 4,008 m out. A player's shot like that is an 8 s blob flying off ahead of the bus. *Fix:* if it's not seen on its first step, end it as `lost` after the same beat.
- **m83. A lock's shot after a pour refills like the Hose, not like a tap.** HOOKS.md:95 says that after a homing shot "the belly then refills as after a tap" (1.6 s). A claim mid-pour closes the Hose, which sets `rechargeSeconds = 3` (`Hose.ts:386`). The lock's shot empties the belly (`Flight.ts:557`), and the refill goes on using those 3 s until the belly is full (`Flight.ts:561`). So the next tap is ready about 4 s after the lift, where after a tap it would be about 2.6. *Fix:* reset `rechargeSeconds` in `tryBarf` when a breath starts, unless whoever starts it sets its own (as the Bomb does after its breath).
- **m84. A tap while the Hose pours does nothing, and makes no sound.** `input.onTap` returns silently while `hose.pouring` (`main.ts:1168`). With one finger pouring and the other thumb in the lane, that thumb's taps vanish: no splat, no cough. The rule elsewhere is "a lift that fires nothing is never silent" (`Hold.ts:429`), and the coach teaches HOLD HIGH UP TO AIM to every new player. *Fix:* cough (`audio.denied()`), as a tap on an empty belly does, or let the tap close the pour and splat.

## Nit

- **n80.** `release()` doesn't tell the owner it has lost the hold (`Hold.ts:241-243`), and `end` reaches only the owner and watchers (`:376`). So if another handler calls `holds.release('hose')`, the Hose isn't told: it pours on past the lift until the tank is dry, and taps are blocked meanwhile. HOOKS.md:44 says a release means "nothing pours". Either call the owner's `lost` on a release, or say in HOOKS.md that only the owner may release.
- **n81.** `Hold.owner`'s comment says it's null "as when the Hose found no belly to pour" (`Hold.ts:66`). But `Hose.start` returns true even when it coughs (`Hose.ts:197-202`), so the owner reads `'hose'`.
- **n82.** `Aimer.fire` calls `comet.shotOf` before anything is known to fire (`Aim.ts:518`), and `shotOf` swaps the side and drops the held landing (`Comet.ts:442-445`). A Bomb that then fizzles on `tryBarf` (`Bomb.ts:213`, `:219`), or a Lob that wins `chooseShot` once it's built, moves the next Comet to the other side for nothing.
- **n83.** `blastCap`'s comment gives `config.bomb.cap` as 900 (`blastLayout.ts:97`). It's 2,400 (`config.ts:902`).

## What holds

- **Holds.** `start` is asked in the order handlers were added, with the Hose last. `move` goes to the owner and to watchers, `end` to the owner and every watcher, `lost` on a claim. Every handler call is caught, and the console is told once per handler. `tap()` is asked before every tap splats, a Bomb's early lift included (`main.ts:1176`). `rayAt`, `screenOf`, `landing`, `inSight` and `bus` don't allocate, and their scratch vectors are safe to call from inside a shot's `target()`.
- **Input.** The hold is armed on one frame and starts on a later one, `aimLagMs` past `aimHoldMs`. A still lift before it starts is a tap (`secondTap`'s Infinity while armed). Every way a finger can go (a lift, a cancel, three fingers, focus lost, a dropped pointer, a hand-over) ends the aim and the Bomb, cancelled unless it was a lift.
- **The Bomb.** II shows at `bomb.two` + `aimLagMs` and III at `bomb.three` + `aimLagMs` after a full-belly landing, on a frame after the one that saw it: 0.69 and 1.29 s at 60 fps, as HOOKS.md says (the hold sim's clunks at 685 and 1,286 ms). A gift goes straight to III at 0.42 s. A bank never goes under the riser. A watcher that heard `tier(1)` always hears one of `release`, `cancel` or `tap`. Stages go up one at a time, never past 3 (`Splat.ts`, `level`, `last`, `more`), and a Bomb never asks the Lift.
- **The Comet.** `plan` has no allocation, every loop in it is bounded, a `clamp` that says false gives the classic shot, and the aim's mover comes back with it. A tile that isn't in yet is planned 0.2 s before the impact, slid with the Moon's map, and refunded on a miss.
- **School Zone and Curb.** A gear put in through `shift(1)` clears the hover (`Turbo.ts`, `shift`), which is what the earlier review's m48 asked for School Zone. Recess's side of that wasn't read. A flick up off Curb, a pull, the lane's end or a thrown gear all lift the wheels through one `leave()`. `ride()` leaves the flight as if it had skimmed there.

## Left out

- **The Comet's cost a frame.** At Comet speed, `plan` runs every frame: up to about 95 lens projections and a few hundred city queries for the arc's march. It's bounded, and BUILT.md times the probe. Not measured here, and not a defect.
- **A Comet landing off the map.** `plan` has no map bounds (`Comet.ts:224-357`), so near the edge at Turbo it could land up to 1.9 km out. The gear's edge fade (`Turbo.ts:394-412`) may bring the speed under 225 m/s first. Not modelled, so it's under "Not checked".
- **A Comet on a wall's face just ahead of the bus.** Once the highest arc can't clear a wall inside `wallAhead` (`Comet.ts:292-321`), it lands on the wall's face as little as 4 m past the launch point, and the bus is past that by the impact. That needs a wall more than about 10 to 20 m over the bus's line within 8 to 16 m ahead, where the gear's floor would already be lifting the bus. Not modelled.
