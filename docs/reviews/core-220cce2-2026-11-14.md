# The core, the release candidate (feat/core 4f33648 to 220cce2)

The second account, 2026-11-14, for the first account. M1's task 4: a code review of `feat/core` from 4f33648 to 220cce2 (54 commits), the build that goes live first. Read against `docs/specs/core/STAGE1.md` on that branch and the four sections of `docs/TODO.md` that hand it over. Lines are at 220cce2. Numbers start at M100, m100 and n100.

**Verdict.** No blocker. Two majors, both in the resting thumb's fix, both new against what is live, both with a small fix that two readers built and ran green. Fix them, then the core is ready for the producer's word as far as this review can see. One gate beside them: the 13 commits under the base that had no review (below). Everything else is minor: nothing found in the loading page, the return visit's rule, the first-draw cap, the tree's clamp or the tags that should hold the release.

- **M100.** With a second finger on the glass that is not steering (a thumb resting on the lane, or a finger in the look zone), the other thumb's tap is planned again between frames, and in a gear it now and then lands off what glinted: in Turbo about 1 such tap in 18 to 40, up to 1.1 km off. ## Major

**M100. A tap beside a finger that is down and not steering is planned again between frames, and in a gear it lands off what glinted.** `src/game/Aim.ts:533`
- *What a player sees.* In a gear, a second finger is on the glass and not steering: a thumb that has just come down on the lane and rests (the tap lands before the rest commits, 450 ms), or a finger in the look zone, still or panning. The other thumb taps. That tap is planned again between frames, and now and then it throws something other than what glinted. In Turbo 1 and 3, beside a thumb resting on the lane, every such tap is replanned and about 1 in 18 lands off its glint, 106 m to 1,090 m away; beside a finger still in the look zone about 1 in 40 (139 m to 1,149 m); beside one panning, 8 of 144. Most are a Comet thrown about a kilometre past a classic target that glinted, and in a twin run the glint stays on that same building for six more frames. At Nonstop and Jet 1 in 36 to 1 in 200, 100 to 152 m off. At Cruise none. With one thumb, or beside a thumb that is steering, none (288 taps, 0 replanned). The base 4f33648, which is what a merge to main replaces: none in any of these.
- *Why.* Before a tap fires `Input` gives back every other finger's press: the flying thumb's landing in `answer()` (`if (this.fly && this.flyPress) this.onUnpress?.(this.flyPress);`, `Input.ts:1019`) and every look finger's in `giveBackRests` (`Input.ts:1044-1051`, every finger since 4899a73). `Aimer.unpress` marks the plan stale whenever the Lob still lists that press (`if (this.lob?.unpress(n)) this.given = true;`, `Aim.ts:533`), and the Lob lists every landing made with a ready belly, the body level or not (`Aim.ts:553`). So with no Lob at stake `refresh()` still runs `update()` again between frames (`Aim.ts:546`). The frame's plan is made before the camera moves (`main.ts:2003`, `rig.update` at 2029), so the replan sees this frame's flight through this frame's lens where every frame sees it through the last frame's, and `Comet.plan`'s in-view test (`Comet.ts:261`) answers differently. 3a2e63f's guard covers only a thumb the Aimer has already seen steer.
- *Evidence.* `p_glint.ts` and `p_glint2.ts` (the real `Input`, `Flight`, `Aimer`, `Comet`, `Lob` and `CameraRig` in `main.ts`'s frame order; what glinted on the last frame against what `fire()` threw). Head, Turbo 3, 30 fps: "wa turbo3@30 none  taps 24 fired 24 replanned 0 off the glint 0"; "wa turbo3@30 still taps 24 fired 24 replanned 17 off the glint 2 (2 of them replanned; the replan moved the plan in 2) (1068, 1053 m) comet>comet tap>tap tap>comet"; "wa turbo3@30 rest  taps 24 fired 24 replanned 24 off the glint 2 ... (1048, 1090 m)". The finger panning, Turbo 1 and 3: "glint probe: 8 taps off what glinted" of 144. The base's src, the same touches: "replanned 0 off the glint 0" on every line. By commit, a look finger: 3a2e63f 0, bf774f4 0, 4899a73 "5 taps off" of 144. My own run is in the table above.
- *No row sees it.* With `Aimer.unpress` made always stale (the state the first account's own review called a blocker) `s_rest`, `s_gesture`, `s_hold`, `s_rest_chain` and the whole of `s_lob` pass. The rows TODO.md cites for it ("rc-glint-rest and rc-glint-look, 192 taps each, none off its glint") are not in the repo, and in the sims a plan made again between frames is the same plan, since nothing draws the camera between the frame's plan and the tap.
- *Fix.* Mark the plan stale only when a Lob is at stake: in `Aimer.unpress` and `repress`, set `given` only if the body the latch holds (the live body if it holds none), before or after the change, reaches `config.lob.from`. Both readers built it: "replanned 0 off the glint 0" on all nine lines (still, drag, rest; Turbo 3, 30 fps; 216 taps) and at Nonstop and Jet, with "rest chain: all 50 checks passed", `s_rest` "all pass", `s_lob` 1t "all 92 passed". Commit a glint row with it: the probe's rig is the piece the sims lack.

**M101. The resting thumb's Lob is still open: a tap that straddles the rest's commit, a slid tap, a flick then a tap.** `src/game/Input.ts:731`, `src/game/Lob.ts:176`
- *What a player sees.* Pull up and lift. (a) The same thumb comes back down on the lane 0.5 s later and rests. The other thumb taps in the look zone, down at 0.90 s and up at 1.00 s after the lift, so the rest turns 450 ms old between the tap's landing and its lift. The tap throws the lift's Lob at 70 degrees with the body at 16. With nobody resting it is a plain splat, and on the base it is a plain splat with the rest too: new with bf12640. (b) A finger lands in the look zone at 0.5 s and stays. The other thumb's tap slides 12 to 26 px on the lane (still a tap), or flicks a gear and then taps within 0.2 s: a Lob at 70 with the body at 10 to 17, where the same touches with no finger in the look zone splat. The base does (b) too. The Lob's ring is up the frame before each of these taps.
- *How much.* A sweep against twins (the rest on the lane at 0.3, 0.5 and 0.7 s; the other thumb's tap landing 160 to 500 ms after it, lasting 60, 100 or 140 ms; 60 fps): on the head "sweep: 31 of 162 differ from their twin", 30 of them a Lob at 70 with the body at 10.3 to 16.6 where the twin splats; on the base 40 of 162. So the round moved the hole more than it closed it, and the docs' rule, "a finger that rests changes nothing ... twin: all 14 throw what their twin throws", does not hold.
- *Why.* Since bf12640 the tap finger's landing beside a thumb not yet committed is a press (`if (!this.fly || !this.pointerDragging) slot.pressN = this.press();`, `Input.ts:731`). When the Aimer first sees the committed thumb, `Lob.unlatch` parks the end before the last press (`const until = this.ranFrom >= 0 ? this.ranFrom : this.latchUntil;`, `Lob.ts:176`): that is the end the resting thumb's own landing ran on (1.3 s), not the lift's (0.8 s). It then forgets every press (`Lob.ts:185`), so the give-back before the tap finds nothing (`Lob.ts:195`) and the parked body is thrown for `quickTapMs` (`Lob.ts:257`). A slid tap commits as the flying thumb (`Input.ts:763`) and parks the same way; after a flick, `latch(..., quick)` (`Aim.ts:364`) puts the parked latch back.
- *Evidence.* `p_chain.ts` (s_rest_chain's harness with new cases). Head: "FAIL  x/commit-park@60  want none got lob 70  tap at 1.000 s, given back first true, ring the frame before true, body 71.4 at the lift 16.2 at the tap", the same at 30 fps; "FAIL  x/slid-look@60 ... 17.1 at the tap [... press 2 at 0.5; press 3 at 0.9; unpress 2 at 0.97; tap at 0.97 lob]" (the give-back is called and changes nothing); "FAIL  x/flick-look@60 ... tap at 1.170 s ... 9.9 at the tap"; the controls with no second finger pass. By commit, the straddle: the base, 9eb19e1 and 3a2e63f "pass  x/commit-park@60  want none got splat"; bf12640, bf774f4 and 4899a73 FAIL. The branch's rows pass with all of this open: every tap in them lifts before the rest commits, and none slides or follows a stroke.
- *Fix.* One line at the top of `Input.commitDrag()` (`Input.ts:1265`): `this.giveBackRests();`. With it: "sweep: 1 of 162 differ from their twin"; the straddle, slid and flick cases pass at 60 and 30 fps; "rest chain: all 50 checks passed", `s_rest` "all pass", "gesture checks: all 860 passed (349 rows)", `s_hold` 41. Its measured cost: over two fuzz seeds 21 wrong Lobs become 3 lost ones (a slid tap held back as pending behind a finger that landed with it splats). The whole fix is in `Lob.unlatch`: park the end before the committing thumb's own press, and keep the presses, so a later give-back still reaches a parked or restored latch. Then three chain cases: a tap that lifts after the commit, a slid tap, a flick then a tap.
- *On its size.* The second reader put this at minor: the ring shows first, the window is about a tenth of a second of the tap's landing for each rest, and the same Lob is the game's own rule a tenth of a second earlier. I keep it major on the project's own scale: it is the symptom the first account's review of this fix called major 2, three rounds went into it, and STAGE1.md's hand-over says it is closed.

**m104. A bloom's arpeggio now keeps time only while frames are short: after a long impact frame its notes start late and together.** `src/audio/SoundGraph.ts:425`
- *What happens.* A shot blooms in a frame that runs long, which is the impact frame itself: STAGE1.md gives the laptop's Comet impact frame as 82.5 ms. The second note is due 67 ms after the impact hook (LOOKAHEAD 12 ms + arpStep 55 ms), but it is not made until the next frame's update, so it is made after its time and Web Audio starts it at once. With a 150 ms impact frame the second and third notes start together; with 250 ms the second to fifth do; a 0.4 s stall after the impact plays four notes at once, and a 0.7 s stall drops two. A late bell also comes in with no attack: music.ts schedules `g.gain.linearRampToValueAtTime(amps[k], t + atks[k])` with attacks of 0.8 to 4 ms, already over. At the base every note was put on the audio clock in the impact frame, so no frame length could move one.
- *Why.* bloomBurst queues every note but the first (`if (i === 0 || this.queued >= QUEUE) this.bloomNote(t + i * step, i, n, h, level); else { const q = this.queued++ ...`), and ringDue holds back the ones queued this frame and makes the others only 62 ms ahead (`if (q >= old || t > now + LOOKAHEAD + NOTE_LEAD)`, NOTE_LEAD 0.05). So a note is made on time only if an update comes within 62 ms of its time, and the second note only if the frame after the impact starts within about 67 ms of the hook. STAGE1.md line 47 says of d82285f "every voice starts at the same audio time": that holds at steady 60, 30 and 20 fps only.
- *Evidence.* My probe drives the real bloomBurst and ringDue with the audio clock stepped by frame lengths (bloomNote replaced by a recorder; the hook 10 ms into the impact frame, update 4 ms into each later one). On the head: "30 fps, steady: ... made after their time 0"; "30 fps, the impact frame 83 ms (the laptop's Comet hit): ... made after their time 1 (the worst 10 ms late) ... starts 0:0 1:65 2:110 3:165 4:220 5:275"; "30 fps, the impact frame 150 ms: ... made after their time 2 (the worst 77 ms late), starting within 5 ms of the note before 1 ... 0:0 1:132 2:132 3:165"; "30 fps, the impact frame 250 ms: ... 0:0 1:232 2:232 3:232 4:232 5:275"; "60 fps, then a stall of 0.4 s right after the impact: ... 0:0 1:55 2:407 3:407 4:407 5:407"; "60 fps, then a stall of 0.7 s right after the impact: notes made 4, lost 2". The same probe against 4f33648's src: every case "made after their time 0 (the worst 0 ms late)", "lost 0".
- *Fix.* Let the one note due before the next update can come (the second: one bell) be made in the frame that blooms, and raise NOTE_LEAD to about 0.1 s so one long frame does not catch the third. Or, when ringDue finds a note already past its time, move it and the rest of its burst later by that much, so the 55 ms spacing holds. Either way, qualify STAGE1.md's "every voice starts at the same audio time".
