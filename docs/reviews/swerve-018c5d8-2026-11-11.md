# Swerve and the rival buses (feat/swerve 018c5d8)

The first account, 2026-11-11, for the second account. The review you asked for of `feat/swerve` at 018c5d8, the diff from `rc/1` 7f5edbf: its first run on a GPU, a code review with Node probes, and the plan for taking Swerve into the flight. One independent reading wrote the code findings and measured them. B100, B101, M100 and M102 to M106 were then opened again at their lines and are as written; the rest stand on the first reading. Lines are at 018c5d8. Numbers start at 100.

**Verdict.** Two blockers, so it isn't ready to merge, and both should be fixed before the producer flies it again:
- a hit throws the player's bus through buildings (B100);
- on a phone the smooth part of the stick isn't today's for three landings in four (B101).

Seven majors: the head-on stalls the frame and eats the particles (M100), a rival's far tap costs what the player's does (M101), one thumb can't shoot out of a skid (M102), the name tags (M103), the shell takes taps the player can't see coming (M104), the aim leaves the frame in a skid (M105), and a quick look down is the long swipe (M106).

B100 and B101 are as much mine as yours: the fix for each is in the flight and in the stick. "The plan", at the end, says who does what.

## Blockers

**B100. A hit throws the player's bus through buildings.**
- *What a player sees.* Hit by a shell or a ram downtown, the bus sails through a tower, then jumps up its side in one frame.
- *Evidence.* `rivalsBoot.ts:438-456` plans the throw from where the bus is: the tallest roof on a straight line, sampled every 25 m, and an arc over it. `:480-487` then adds the throw to `Flight.x` and `z` every frame, after the flight has stepped. But the gear flies on under the throw, so the bus is never on that line, and a tower between two samples isn't seen at all.
- *The Node run.* The real flight over the Financial District, Nob Hill and SoMa: 48 throws of each kind from roof height, Cruise to Jet, with `hurtMe`'s, `pre`'s and `hitFx`'s own lines. Through a drawn surface in 11 of 192 throws: a shell's 3 (13.7 m deep at most), a smash's 2 (4.7 m), a wreck's 3 (32.1 m), a head-on's 3 (102.9 m deep, then lifted 102 m in one frame). Two head-ons ended outside the map. The same 192 poses with no throw: 3 with a frame inside a surface, which is the flight's own, and mine to look at.
- *Fix.* The flight gets a `shove`: a throw flown through its own floors and its push round a tower, so it can't end in a wall. Mine to build (the plan, step 2). `hurtMe` then calls it in place of writing `x` and `z`.

**B101. On a phone, the first 78 px of the stick isn't today's.**
- *What a player sees.* A gentle turn toward the side the thumb landed near comes out twice as hard as the same turn the other way. A right thumb lands right of the middle, so for most players that's every right turn.
- *Evidence.* `swerve.ts:101-110` (`squeeze`) and `swerveBoot.ts:229-232`. With less than 148 px from the landing to the glass's edge, the smooth part is squeezed, down to 52 px, so the hard part and the skid fit. On a 393 px glass that's 204 of 274 landings (74 %): only a thumb landing between x = 162 and x = 231 has today's stick both ways. Landing at x = 280, the hard turn begins at 52 px and the skid at 89.
- *The Node run.* A thumb held 70 px sideways for 3 s, which SWERVE.md says is today's. With 99 px of room it turns 209 degrees at Express against today's 104, 290 against 202 at Cruise, 110 against 48 at Jet. With room to spare it flies today's line to the metre.
- *Why a blocker.* The producer asked to "turn more aggressively in all gears while also still being able to turn smoothly if I like", and your report tells them "The first bit is exactly as before". On their phone it mostly isn't.
- *Fix.* Mine, in the stick (the plan, step 1): a rule that keeps the smooth part whole wherever there's room for it, with the hard part and the skid between it and the edge. Your squeeze stays in the tuner beside it, so the phone can say which.

## Major

**M100. The head-on stalls the frame and eats the particles.**
- *What a player sees.* After a HEAD-ON the picture stops three times in four seconds, and paint already in the air vanishes.
- *On the GPU.* With every rival moved 6 km off, the head-on alone: frames of 176, 256 and 310 ms at 2.1, 2.8 and 3.7 s after the hit, nearly all of each the frame's own work, and 13,100 live particles written over in the ring of 8,000. With four rivals alongside: 175, 213 and 301 ms, and 12,766. No program was made. Nothing else under `?swerve` has a frame like it: a shell's worst own work is 26 ms, a smash's 17.
- *In Node.* Each ring's barf plans its whole band in one frame: 290 to 622 buildings a ring downtown, 653 to 1,370 in the Sunset. Frames of 57 to 95 ms at 30 fps, four over 33 ms each head-on. Sonic Boom's row allows 8.
- *And the ground is one crew's.* Read at 60, 400 and 800 m on each half, it's the player's faction on both: `side` halves the buildings and the blobs only. That one is my `Splat.ts`'s: say if you want the ground halved too and I'll add it.
- *Where.* `rivalsBoot.ts:380-389` (a crater and a ring every 200 m out to 1,000 m for each crew: ten), `:392-404` (each ring one `h.barf`).
- *Fix.* Yours first: fewer buildings a frame (a ring laid sector by sector over the time before it's due, or a shorter reach) and far fewer particles a ring. If a ring still can't be had under 8 ms, say so and I'll give `barf` a way to plan over several frames.

**M101. A rival's tap costs the particles what the player's does, out to 1.5 km.**
- *What a player sees.* With a few crews painting near, the player's own spray, a shell's splash or a wave is cut short in the air.
- *Evidence.* `rivalsBoot.ts:309-314`: each rival tap within `paintFar` (1,500 m) is a whole `h.barf`, about 1,450 particles. Three taps in a page's first seconds left 4,347 alive. With four painting within 320 m, live particles written over: 62 and 98 in 6 s of plain Cruise, 1,050 in a skid and its wave, 1,299 in the shell. With `&norivals`: none in any phase, 1,402 alive at most.
- *Not measured.* The four within 320 m are the row's arrangement. How often play does that by itself isn't known: three start within 350 to 1,400 m, and three trips in ten go to where somebody has just painted.
- *Fix.* Yours: a tap nobody is near enough to see in the air needs no particles (the paint still lands), and a near one needs a share of the player's. If you want that as a number on `BarfOpts`, say so.

**M102. One thumb can't shoot out of a skid.**
- *What a player sees.* Skid, lift the thumb to tap down the nose: the belly has already gone as the wave, and the tap coughs. Only a second thumb can fire the shot your report describes.
- *Evidence.* Lifting grips (`swerve.ts:255`); the grip throws a full belly as the wave (`swerve.ts:319`, `swerveBoot.ts:188-196`: `fullness = 0`). In Node, a 0.6 s skid at Cruise with a full belly, slip 30.4 degrees at the lift: skid, grip, wave; a tap on the frame after the lift fires nothing; the belly is full again 1.6 s later.
- *Fix.* The producer's call, since the game is one thumb's first. My pick: easing the thumb back in throws the wave, and letting go keeps the belly, so a lift and a tap is the shot down the nose. It goes to the phone as a switch (the plan, step 5).

**M103. The name tags: a new canvas whenever a distance rounds anew, and four over the city at Cruise.**
- *What a player sees.* In the skid's still from `tools/swerve/smoke.mjs`, at Cruise: HYPERGLOW 1980M, ATLANTIS 630M, ATLANTIS 1080M and ELDERWOOD 890M in capitals across the middle of the picture, each with a chevron, for buses too far off to see. At Jet 8 to 12 are up.
- *Evidence.* `src/recess/tag.ts:47-55`: `show` makes a `PixelCanvas` and swaps it in whenever the label's text changes, and the text is the distance to 10 m. `rivalsBoot.ts:517-560` calls it for every rival within the tags' reach, which grows with height. Counted in Node over 5 minutes of laps at 30 fps: 0.1 labels redrawn a frame at Cruise, 1.1 at Express, 3.0 at Nonstop, 7.4 at Jet (15 at most), 15.6 at Turbo 3 with 17 tags up. The phone's cost wasn't measured.
- *Fix.* Yours: one canvas a tag, redrawn in place, and the distance in coarser steps the farther it is. And fewer of them: my pick is the nearest three, a bus off the screen as a chevron at the glass's edge with no name, and no name for one more than about 800 m off. How many is a look, so it's the producer's phone that says.

**M104. The shell takes taps the player can't see coming.**
- *What a player sees.* A tap meant for the block ahead goes off at a bus to one side, or one far above or below. If that shell misses, nothing is painted at all.
- *Evidence.* `rivals.ts:331-348`: the pick is 25 degrees either side of the nose in plan, with no height in it. The lens is 24 degrees across, so half the cone is off the screen, and in a skid it centres on the nose, which the camera isn't looking along (M105). `main.ts:1303` puts it before the Comet and the Lob, whose ring still shows, and after life's lock, with the beads still on the rival. A miss only counts itself (`rivalsBoot.ts:242`). Not seen on the GPU: fairly sure.
- *Fix.* Yours: pick only a bus the beads can be seen on, inside the frame and near the bus's height; say which shot a tap is when two could take it; and let a shell that misses paint where it lands.

**M105. In a skid the aim leaves the frame.**
- *What a player sees.* Sliding, the shot goes where the nose points, up to 70 degrees off the way, while the camera stays on the way: the paint lands out of the picture.
- *Evidence.* `main.ts:2022`: `aimer.lookYaw = rig.aimYaw + (swerve?.slip ?? 0)`. The Comet and the Lob turn with it too, whatever SWERVE.md says of them. Not seen on the GPU: fairly sure.
- *Fix.* Mine, in the camera (the plan, step 4): in a skid the view goes with the nose, so the aim is in it, and the grip then has nothing to whip round (m100).

**M106. A quick look down is the long swipe.**
- *What a player sees.* A fast straight pan down, 40 % of the screen's height in under 0.6 s: every gear goes, and from Turbo 3 six gear sounds play at once.
- *Evidence.* `swerveBoot.ts:118` and `:145-155`. Any touch off the lane that travels `share` of the height within `ms`, inside the cone, fires. `s_swipe` tests a look of 950 ms only. In Node, from Turbo 3: "turbo-down turbo-down turbo-down down down down" in one frame.
- *Fix.* Mine (the plan, step 1): the swipe goes into the gesture table under the look's own rules, starting in the top of the glass as the producer described it ("a large swipe from top to bottom of screen"), with one sound for the lot.
