# The Lob, the review fixes and the picks (feat/shots fd6d413 to fa91865)

The second account, 2026-11-09, for the first account. A code review of `feat/shots` from fd6d413 to fa91865: the Lob as a whole (`Lob.ts` and its wiring), the fixes made after the reviews of fd6d413 and of the extras, and the one-number picks. Read against BUILT.md, HOOKS.md and SHOTS.md (on `feat/earth`). Each finding was opened at its file:line and tried against the code; five have a Node run behind them. Lines are at fa91865. The other minors: the view's tip on pages without the live sky, a fourth one-time hint the plan doesn't have, three gaps in HOOKS.md, and two things made every frame.

## Major

**M90. The Lob's latch never has to end.**
- *What a player sees.* Pull up at Express, lift, tap: a Lob. Keep tapping while the belly refills. When it's full, with the bus flying level, the ring comes up again and the tap throws a second Lob 1.2 km off, where a splat on the block ahead was meant. Or: pull, lift, flick up a gear, tap within 0.8 s: a Lob at the pull's angle, the nose long down. HOOKS.md and SHOTS.md say a Lob is a tap with the body more than 25 degrees up, and `Lob.ts` gives the latch 0.8 s. The ring does show each time.
- *Evidence.* Three doors, one cause: nothing bounds `latchUntil`.
  - A press inside a latch runs it on 0.8 s from the press, every time (`Lob.ts:121-124`). `Aimer.press` asks above its `ready` gate (`Aim.ts:534`, the gate at `:542`), so taps on an empty belly count.
  - A press with no latch live latches the body as it is (`Lob.ts:125-127`). `fired()` drops the latch (`:396`) while the body is still up: 37 degrees at Express 0.23 s after the throw. So the first tap after a Lob starts a new latch, and the next ones keep it.
  - A stroke lifted within 150 ms of committing gets the latch it parked back with a new 0.8 s (`Lob.ts:113-119`, `Aim.ts:359`). Every flick is such a stroke. `fired()` leaves `parkedAt` set too.
- *The Node runs.* The real Flight, Aimer, Comet and Lob over the Western Addition, the press and the thumb given as `main.ts` gives them. The same outcome at 30 and 60 fps.
  - `t3.ts`, taps every 0.3 s after a Lob at Express: the second fires 2.7 s on, the body at -0.6 degrees, the ring up: "lob at 37.2 deg, 1242 m out". Every 0.5 s: 27.6 degrees. Every 0.7 s, and at Cruise: a plain splat.
  - `t1.ts`, Cruise, lifted at 57.1 degrees. No stroke and a tap 0.9 s on: a plain splat. One 0.1 s stroke, then a tap 1.2 s after the lift, the body at 7.2: "lob at 57.1 deg, 1417 m out". Eight strokes 0.7 s apart: 6.2 s after the lift, the body at 0.1, still a Lob at 57.1.
- *Fix.* Keep the time the body was captured, and never let `latchUntil` pass it by more than twice `config.lob.latch`. Give a parked latch back with the end it had. Move the press's latch under the `ready` gate, and clear `parkedAt` in `fired()`. Then two `s_lob` rows, each a plain splat: a flick after the lift, and taps mashed after a Lob at Express.

## Minor

**m90. M71's fix lets go too soon for a Bomb from high up.**
- *What a player sees.* With `&bomb&falls`, a Bomb III from 450 m or more onto a tower whose roof is under about 100 m: a hitch 1.6 to 1.9 s after it lands.
- *Evidence.* The pool waits for a wide blast, but at most `poolWait`, 1.2 s, past the runs' reaching the street (`Falls.ts:169`, `:36`). From a roof 40 m up that's 1.65 s after the impact. A Bomb III's ground is due at 1.57 s from 300 m, 1.96 from 450 and 2.35 from 600 (`groundDeadline`). Begun before that, the pool finishes the rest whole (`GroundPaint.ts:702`).
- *The Node run* (`m71.ts`, the game's own functions): from 600 m onto a roof 40 m up, 37 % of the circle is still to draw, 245 thousand m2; 60 m up, 25 %. BUILT.md's 24-37 ms frame was a whole 306 m circle, 294 thousand. `s_falls`'s row is a 128 m tower, which waits. No frame was measured.
- *Fix.* Wait for the wide job's own `due`, not a fixed 1.2 s. Add a row from 600 m onto a 40-60 m roof.

**m91. The Lob's tip doesn't come down on pages without the live sky.**
- *What a player sees.* On `?moon`, `?sky=off` and `?hour=`, the view tips up after the star and stays at its highest until the hit. The comment says it comes down as the star falls.
- *Evidence.* `main.ts:1904` takes the larger of the target as it was and the new one. Only the live sky sets it back each frame (`:1895`); without it, it goes to 0 only when no Lob is up (`:1906`). From the code.
- *Fix.* `Math.max(live ? rig.skyLiftTarget : 0, ...)`.

**m92. The Lob's hint is a fourth one-time hint.**
- SHOTS.md approves three in context: the first boat, before the first Bomb, the first low pass over a road (`SHOTS.md:294`, `:364`). TAP TO LOB IT / INTO THE RING is one more (`Coach.ts:156-159`, `Controls.ts:405-407`). BUILT.md describes it as built, not as a call with our pick, so the producer's approval of the picks doesn't cover it.
- *Fix.* Put it to the producer in BUILT.md, or take it out.

**m93. HOOKS.md leaves out three things the hook now does.**
- `Hold.late` (`Hold.ts:69`, `:402`) isn't in the Hold table. `cancelled` is listed as a lost touch, three fingers or lost focus (`HOOKS.md:55`). With the Lob's ring up, a late lift also ends the hold cancelled, and `tap()` is asked in the same event.
- A Lob latched when `comet.clamp` turns false is still thrown. `t5.ts`, the clamp set as the thumb lifts and a tap 0.37 s on, at Cruise and Express: "clampAsked":0, "tap":"lob". The clamp is asked only when a landing is picked afresh (`Lob.ts:178`, `:264`): for 0.8 s it isn't, 1.6 with a press, and longer until M90 is fixed. HOOKS.md gives the Comet's 0.5 s and nothing for the Lob. Set before the pull, no Lob is planned.
- "The Lob" says it lands 1.03-1.6 km off (`HOOKS.md:163`, `:172`). In `s_lob` a wall in its climb takes it 209 m out, and the code moves it out to 1.9 km.
- *Fix.* Three lines in HOOKS.md.

**m94. Two things are made every frame.**
- `main.ts:2124` builds an object for `Object.assign(lobRing, ...)` every frame a Lob is up, 1.4-3.3 s each.
- `Comet.ts:87` makes `chooseShot`'s `far` closure every frame both a Comet and a Lob are planned.
- *Fix.* Set the five fields; a plain function.
