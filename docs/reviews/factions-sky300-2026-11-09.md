# The sky pieces at 300 m (feat/factions 9a183a1 to e2712fd)

The second account, 2026-11-09, for the first account. A code review of the nine commits since my last one: the sky pieces raised to 300 m over the heart's ground and over the highest floating roof under each (`src/world/liftSky.ts`, `skyY`, `SkyPlace.roof`), the wave's heights placed ahead of it so the pieces can stand over them (`src/world/Lift.ts`, `plan`, `roofNear`, `roofGrid`), and the pieces cut round the bus (`sbCut`, `uSkyBus`). Read against LIFT.md's call 5 and its notes after the review of fbd3033.

**Verdict.** No blocker and no major. Two minors, both about the cut round the bus, and three nits. The heights hold as built: a piece is over every roof the plan can put under it, the plan places the wave's houses in the wave's own order with the wave's own `liftPlace`, and a house hit before or after the plan keeps a height its neighbours were placed against. It's ready for the producer's eye, with m90 measured on the GPU first.

## Minors

**m90. The cut's `discard` is in every fragment of every piece, cut or not** (`src/world/liftSky.ts`, `sbCut` in `FS_HEAD`, called first in all four `main`s). On Apple's GPUs a draw whose shader can discard is kept out of hidden-surface removal, so wherever a two-sided piece covers the screen every layer of it is shaded: the Sky Sea's belly and its top, both sides of the black sun, the smoke over it. From under the Sea the piece is most of the frame. I couldn't measure it here. For Wednesday's GPU rows: the frame's cost under each piece from the street and from 250 m, against the same with the cut's reach at 0. If it costs, the pieces that never cross the line to the bus from the street (the smoke's ceiling, the Skyway from below) could go without the call.

**m91. The cut is an angle from the lens, so it's the same size on the screen whatever the distance** (`SKY_CUT` 0.11, `sbCut`'s `a < uSkyBus.w`). At the street's lens (`hfovPortrait` 24 degrees, a half width of 0.21) that's a hole 52 % of the frame's width, whole across 35 %, wherever a piece crosses the line to the bus. From the chase view that's the 25 m LIFT.md means. From a lens that stands far off (the Bomb's pull-back, a wide vista, a pinch out when that's built) it's still half the frame, round a bus a few pixels long. Capping the cone at the bus in metres would keep it the size it was designed at: `min(uSkyBus.w, 25.0 / lb)`.

## Nits

- **n90.** `uSkyBus` starts at y -1e6 and `Lift.frame` sets it before `skyStep` adds a finished piece (`Lift.ts`, `this.sky?.bus(...)` at the top of `frame`, `this.sky = piece` later in the same frame). So a piece's first frame is drawn with the default: with the lens over the piece then, that's a round bite straight down for one frame. Call `piece.bus(...)` where the piece is set.
- **n91.** `&skyy=` is read from the page's address at the module's load, in the shipped build. It's QA's, and LIFT.md says so; a line by `skyOver` that a value under the layer's top puts a piece in the houses would save the next reader a check.
- **n92.** `planned` is never cleared and `plan()` runs once. That's right while a page makes one Lift for one neighbourhood; a second `stage()` would lift the first plan's houses at stale heights.

## What I checked and found right

- `skyY` over an empty list or a place with no floating house is the 300 m (`-Infinity` from `roof` leaves it).
- `roofNear`'s cells take the square round the disc asked for, never less; `roofCells` is dropped at each `buildSky`.
- A house scheduled before the plan is skipped by it and counted by its neighbours' placing; one scheduled after keeps its planned height; `nearPlaced` counts a planned house not yet up.
- The risers' feet are still picked for 220 m (`FOOT_Y`): the pieces standing higher only leaves them more room.
- `sbCut` leaves alone what's behind the lens and what's more than 10 m past the bus's middle, and guards a lens at the bus.
