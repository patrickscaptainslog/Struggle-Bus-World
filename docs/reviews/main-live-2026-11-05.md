## Minors

**m1. At twilight the first paint and the game's page colour disagree** (`index.html:56` against `LiveSky.ts:252, 260-271`). The boot script picks one of three colours by the Sun's height (grey from 3 degrees, `#5a4f9c` down to -1, `#394295` under that); `LiveSky.page()` writes the blended table's SKY_LOW 3, which is continuous. Run through `TableBlend`: at dusk at 0 degrees the boot paints `#5a4f9c` and the game writes `#aa78a2`; at -3, `#394295` against `#6d559e`; at +6 the boot leaves the grey and the game writes `#d6bbb0`; at dawn +2, `#5a4f9c` against `#aaa3b9`. They agree only over about 14.5 degrees at dusk (8.5 at dawn) and under -5. So on a visit within about two hours of sunset the status bar and the page jump from dark violet to light mauve when `city.json` lands. Fix: the boot carries a few SKY_LOW 3 colours by the Sun's height, setting and rising. (The agent ran it.)

**m2. `lamps.bin` loads on every page, by day too** (`main.ts:102`, `:201`; `lampLoad.ts:7`). With the live sky `hour` is always set, so the lamps' 94 KB are fetched behind `far.bin` at default priority while the opening's regions are still streaming, and `setPools` stamps and uploads 16 KB a loaded tile, a tile a frame, at noon too, when the pools add nothing. The stars' and the Moon's 112 KB were put after `cityIn` for exactly this. Fix: low priority after `cityIn`, or when the Sun nears -2 degrees. (Checked by reading, both of us; not measured on a slow line.)

**m3. One tree swatch is taupe for a minute or so after sunrise** (`SkyTable.ts:192`). The brown gate is tested on the floats; GREEN_GREY step 7 comes out at L just over 0.72, so it isn't clamped, and rounded to bytes it's `#aea38f` (L 0.719, C 0.031, hue 82.6), which skycheck's taupe test fails. It's reached with the Sun rising through 3.0-3.2 degrees, every morning in every city, on sunlit tree tops at step 7. Fix: gate the rounded bytes, or a small margin. (The agent ran the real `TableBlend` from 80 to -20 degrees in 0.01 steps: that's the only reachable brown in a row the street draws; CLOUD_SHADE fails too, but nothing on main draws it.)

**m4. Stars and planets show through the Moon's dark side** (`skyResolve.ts:122`). The stars are resolved first, so on the Moon's unlit side the sky's colour is already the star's, and the Moon keeps it unless earthshine outshines it; its comment says it "covers the stars". Nine nights in San Francisco from November 2026 to December 2027 put a star the game draws inside the drawn disc on its dark side, the Moon up and the Sun under -5: one is 2026-12-02T10:10Z, a magnitude 4.2 star 0.22 degrees from the Moon's centre, drawn as a GLOW_WHITE dot on the dark side of a Moon 37 % lit. Fix: the disc's test before the stars', or the dark side taking the sky's own colour. (The agent searched the dates; I read the order.)

**m5. A breath from under the Golden Gate's deck, with nothing glinting, still takes the bridge up a level** (`Splat.ts`, `barf`: the reach is a flat distance). In `tools/bridge-paint.mjs`'s aim rows on main's build: from under the deck the glint and the target are null and the breath lands on the water, as the tool says it should, but the bridge goes from level 2 to 3, its rainbow road. The deck is 67 m over the impact and inside the blast's flat reach, so it's dealt as a neighbour. A player under the bridge with nothing glinting gets its biggest change. Fix: a landmark whose surface is far over the impact (a deck seen from under) isn't in its reach, as `from` already keeps the landing off it. (Ran, `bridge-paint.mjs`.)

**m6. With the bus behind the lens the lane falls back to the left 42 %** (`main.ts:1414`, `input.busZone = v.z < 1 ? busLane : null`). A look far enough round puts the bus behind the camera, and the rule becomes the old one for those frames: a thumb that landed under where the bus was looks instead of flying. On `feat/earth` the lane stays where the bus was last on the screen (c11baeb, the first review's minor, merged with main's lane at 788f88a); main can take the same three lines.

**m7. `promote()` reads the lane at the lift, not at the landing** (`Input.ts:881`, `this.inLookZone(s.x0, s.y0)`). A touch that landed out of the lane during a lone flick is promoted to fly if the bus has moved under it by the flick's lift. On `feat/earth` a slot keeps whether it landed in the look zone (`Slot.inLook`), and the gesture sim has two rows for it, the second failing on main's `Input.ts` (merged at 788f88a).

**Trivial.** The tuner shows the sky section on every page, but its `sky.live` row does nothing: `flag.ts:18` reads `config.sky.live` as the module loads, before `applySavedTuning()` (`main.ts:56`). Developers only.

## For whoever merges main and `feat/earth` next

Not main's faults, but they'll come back with every merge. Main's street Moon and Sun disc declare `tMoonMap`, `uMoonSun` (a `vec4`), `uMoonRot`, `uMoonLod` and `uSunDisc` (a disc in buffer pixels), and `feat/earth`'s space branch declares the same five names (`uMoonSun` a `vec3`, `uSunDisc` a direction and a cosine). `?earth` compiles both into the street's resolve, so the shader didn't compile (`trip.mjs` found it). On `feat/earth` main's five are `tSkyMoon`, `uSkyMoon*` and `uSkySunDisc` (788f88a, 98bc848). Taking those names on main too would end it. Main's tools don't have `tools/sky/serve-dist.mjs`; its tools served by `feat/earth`'s ran fine.
