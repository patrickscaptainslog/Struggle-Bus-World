# Life on the GPU: planes and people (feat/life, d271fb5)

The first account, 2026-11-05, on the laptop's GPU. Nobody had run `feat/life` on a real GPU: the second account's machine draws with SwiftShader. This is `LIFE.md`'s tests as they stand, L6's laptop leg (`tools/warmcheck.mjs` with `?life`, which `LIFE.md` lists as not yet done) and a real-time flight. It's not a code review. That's owed when the stage hands in.

**Verdict.** Every test passes on the GPU, nothing compiles in flight with `?life`, and the plain page is the page without life. The planes cross 15 km to orbit and back with no jump. Two minors and two nits are below. One major is for the tools: no frame they take shows a headline moment as a player sees it (a person glinting or dancing, a painted plane's streak, level 3's rainbow, a beacon at night, a painted dot from orbit). So the producer's phone is the first to see them.

## How it ran

- `feat/life` at d271fb5, detached in a worktree of its own (`node_modules` a junction, the dependencies being `feat/earth`'s). Two builds:
  - `npx vite build`: main chunk 1,042.76 kB, 329.32 kB gzip;
  - with `SB_NO_LIFE=1`: 1,042.30 kB, 329.12 kB.

  `tsc -p tsconfig.main.json --noEmit` prints nothing, and `iosfloor` says "iOS 15.0 parses every chunk".
- Edge headless via `tools/browser.mjs`, with hardware GL: `ANGLE (Intel, Intel(R) UHD Graphics 620 (0x00005917) Direct3D11 vs_5_0 ps_5_0, D3D11)`, Chromium 156. Each browser ran alone, started with 701 to 1,280 MB free. So the real-time numbers are a busy laptop's. Counts, programs and positions aren't.
- The logs, frames and the real-time script stay on the laptop (`sb-checks/life-gpu-2026-11-05/`), not in the repo. `vectors.mjs` rewrote `src/life/checkVectors.json` with only its engine string changed (V8 12.4 to 13.6, the vectors the same); it was restored.

## Findings

**M1, a major for the tools: no frame shows a headline moment as a player sees it.**
- `paint.mjs:89` takes `person-glint.png` two frames after the bus is pinned, before the camera follows, so the bus isn't in it.
- `:102` takes the dance at its frame 6, under the breath's own spray.
- `:226` takes the plane at its hit, when the contrail has no length yet.
- `sky.mjs` takes its frames in Turbo 3. There, the turbo's own speed lines (`src/fx/Turbo.ts:346-348`, one in five with a hue) can't be told from life's specks and contrails, and the same lines show without life.

So the glint, the dance, the streak, level 3's rainbow, the beacon and the painted dot to orbit are proved by numbers only. Frames taken as a player sees them would let the next review look: the bus in the frame after the camera has followed, the dance once the spray is gone, the streak a few seconds after the hit, the sky out of Turbo 3.

**m1: in the browser a few people stand off their cells, up to 0.71 m off the ground.**
- `smoke.mjs`'s people row reads `{"people":485,"near":90,"models":448,"specks":42,"off":13,"worstYm":0.71,"busY":6}`. That's 1 in 37 off a sidewalk, plaza or path cell, and the worst 0.71 m off the ground, against Node's 0 and 0.05 m.
- It passes the tool's own limits (`smoke.mjs:71`: 5 % and 1 m). So the gap is against the loaded city's `groundClassAt` and `groundAt`, not the worker's arithmetic.
- Where to look: the height between the cells' middles on 4 m cells (`src/life/sim/people.ts`), or the host's carry-on by velocity (`src/life/Life.ts`). Up close, at a kerb or a step, that's 40 % of a person in the road or the ground.

**m2: life's draws and triangles weren't measured, and by the code it can be 5 draws against LIVING.md's 3.**
- The real-time script read `renderer.info` between frames, after three's reset, so it read 0: this account's miss.
- The fps overlay can't be subtracted, because the runs saw different scenes. At 20 s it read 30 draws and 270 K triangles with life, against 32 and 246 K without; at 40 s, 19 and 226 K against 18 and 245 K.
- By the code, life is up to five draws, each on when anything in it is live (`pools.ts:251`, `:294`, `:333`): three pools, the specks and the marks.
- The people pool's one model is 8 boxes, 96 triangles a person, half folded away. At the 528 people seen at night that's about 50 K triangles, under 80 K. That's a count from the code, not a measurement.

L3 waits for the boats and cars anyway; this is for when it comes.

**n1: at night only a speck has a beacon.** `pools.ts:165-167` blinks a grey plane only while it's a speck. One near enough to be a model shows no light, where a real plane's beacon shows close up too. That's the second account's call. It's here so it's a choice.

**n2: one 167 ms frame in `warmcheck`'s blasts with `?life`.** It's the only frame of its kind: the plain page has none there. The real-time runs had two over 100 ms without life and none with it, so on this laptop it reads as the machine. `life.blast` is one loop over the snapshot's rows.
