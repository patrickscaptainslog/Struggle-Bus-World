## Minor

**m400. a1ecc30: a tap that slides, landing late in the lift's 0.8 s with the ring up, now throws a plain splat (a Lob at fa91865, and a Lob if the finger stays still).** `src/game/Lob.ts:152`
- *What happens.* Cruise or Express, one thumb. Pull, lift. The tap's finger lands 0.7 to 0.8 s after the lift, the Lob's ring up; it slides past the 12 px tap slop (a steering stroke for Input) and that commit is first seen by a frame after 0.8 s; it lifts inside quickTapMs, still a tap. The ring's plan goes on the commit frame and the tap throws a plain splat on the block ahead, the ring still fading on screen (main.ts:2120 fades it at 6 a second; read only). The same finger held still throws the Lob, and so did the sliding one at fa91865. The window is the last 0.05 to 0.1 s of the latch, so it is rare.
- *Why.* `const until = this.ranFrom >= 0 ? this.ranFrom : this.latchUntil; if (t < until) {` parks the latch only while the end from before the stroke's own landing (0.8 s) is still ahead. A slid tap's own landing ran the latch on, as any tap's does, but once 0.8 s has passed nothing is parked, latchUntil goes to -1, and the last frame before the lift plans from the live body. A flick and a slid tap are the same stroke until the lift; a1ecc30 treats both as the flick.
- *Evidence.* Probe, 4f33648: "cruise/slid/land0.74-commit0.82-lift0.87@60 lift 71.4 | 0.88 s: splat, the body 19.1 | ring 0.02-0.83 s"; "cruise/slid/land0.7-commit0.81-lift0.84@60 ... 0.85 s: splat, the body 20 | ring 0.02-0.82 s"; "express/slid/land0.74-commit0.82-lift0.87@60 ... splat, the body 11.3"; the same at 30 fps. Its still twin: "cruise/still/land0.78-lift0.9@60 ... 0.9 s: LOB at 70 deg, 1016.8 m out, the body 18.7 | ring 0.02-0.9 s". Commit before 0.8 s is still a Lob ("land0.7-commit0.78-lift0.84@60 ... LOB at 70 deg"). On fa91865's src all three late rows are Lobs ("cruise/slid/land0.74-commit0.82-lift0.87@60 ... 0.88 s: LOB at 70 deg, 1017.3 m out, the body 19.1"). On origin/feat/core (220cce2) the same rows are splats still ("... 0.88 s: splat, the body 19.1"): its unlatch has the same two lines, and its review would not have seen it, the fault being older than its base. s_lob's slid rows land 0.1 s after the lift only.
- *Fix.* In Lob.unlatch park while the latch as run on is live, and keep the end from before the landing for the give-back: `if (t < this.latchUntil) {` with `this.parkedUntil = until` as it is. Tried in my copy: the late slid rows are Lobs again ("cruise/slid/land0.74-commit0.82-lift0.87@60 ... LOB at 70 deg, 1017.3 m out"), the lane flick is still a splat ("cruise/laneflick0.5-tap1.2@60 ... splat"), and s_lob prints "136 rows in 84 s", "lob checks: all 1231 passed". Add a slid row with gap 0.74. It applies to feat/core as well.
- *The second reader (confirmed).* Reproduced with real touches, and my fuzz found it twice without being aimed at it. The tap's own landing runs the latch on (ranFrom = the lift's 0.8 s); when the frame that first sees the slide's commit comes after that end, Lob.ts:151-152 (`const until = this.ranFrom >= 0 ? this.ranFrom : this.latchUntil; if (t < until) {`) parks nothing and line 159 drops the latch, so the last frame before the lift plans from the live body (19 to 20 degrees) and the tap is a plain splat.

**m402. a1ecc30 needs a frame to see the flick: a lane flick shorter than a frame (or inside one slow frame) keeps its landing's run-on, and the tap 1.2 s after the lift is still a Lob with the bus level; closed on feat/core.** `src/game/Aim.ts:362` Older than this range.

About 0.5 s later a quick flick up a gear in the lane, 70 px in 30 to 40 ms, its commit and its lift both between two frames (or any flick inside one slow frame). Then tap, the finger landing 1.2 s after the lift: a Lob at 70 degrees 964 m out, the body at 3.5, the ring up all the way. ## Nit

**n400. c34034d's ready gate: a finger that lands while the belly is still filling does not run the latch on, so the ring can come up under it and go before a slow lift.** `src/game/Aim.ts:536`
- Pull while the belly refills, lift. The tap's finger lands 0.6 s after the lift, the belly not yet full; it fills 0.1 s later and the ring comes up under the finger; the finger lifts at 0.85 s. The lift's 0.8 s has ended, so the ring went two frames earlier and the tap is a plain splat. At fa91865 that landing ran the latch on and the tap was a Lob. It needs the belly to fill while a tap's finger is down and the lift's 0.8 s to end in the same moment, so it is rare.
- *Fix.* Leave it, or note it in BUILT.md as the gate's price. If it is ever seen on the phone: remember a landing refused for the belly alone, and run the latch on from that landing's time on the frame the belly fills with the finger still down.
- *The second reader (confirmed).* Reproduced with real touches and the real belly (the first reviewer set the belly by hand; mine fills by Flight's own clock, 2.78 s after the first Lob's lift). A finger that lands 0.6 s after the second lift with the belly not yet full is refused by Aim.ts:536's `this.ready`, nothing looks again when the belly fills under it, the ring shows for 0.14 s and the lift's 0.8 s ends before a slow lift: a plain splat where fa91865 threw the Lob. ## What holds

Each as the Lob review asked, by the rows above or by reading.
- **M90, the latch that never ended** (c34034d, a1ecc30): a press runs a latch on at most twice `config.lob.latch` past when the body was latched, and only with the belly ready. The mash cases at 0.2, 0.3 and 0.5 s, Cruise and Express, 60, 30 and 20 fps: the first tap a Lob, every later one a splat (on fa91865 the same probe throws a second Lob: "express/mash0.3@60 ... 2.88 s: LOB at 36.1 deg, 1240.4 m out, the body -0.7"). A flick in the lane then a tap: a splat. What it left open is M400 and m402, both closed on the core.
- **n91, `Lob.unfired`** (71830cc): a Lob's tap that does not leave gets its latch and its landing back, and is not counted.
- **m90, the Waterfall's pool** (2ada8c0): it waits for a wide blast's own due; the new `s_falls` row holds it at 30 fps.
- **m91, m94, n90, n92** (9ff102b, 297fa5c, 2bfc1dc, bd7561c): the tip on pages without the live sky, nothing made every frame for the ring, `lobRingK` gone, the ring on a crater by `CraterPaint.tapR`. Read only: no Node row runs them, and they are right as read.
