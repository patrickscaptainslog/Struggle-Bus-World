- **Step 7, Recess's brain.** Possessed is the default and rides at Nonstop; a flick down wheezes NO BRAKES, the driver keeps the steering, and there are 8 hiccups on beats, on grey only. - **The restart, as BUILT.md reads it:** a crack and a heal each restart the five seconds; three encores and three parries don't (the siege rows).

## Decision 16: the first account's reading

FACTIONS.md §6, as approved: "16. An owner's hit heals a crack, and a hit on stage 4 or 5 restarts the five-second wait. *Pick: yes and yes.*"

- **The restart: BUILT.md's reading stands.** "A hit on stage 4 or 5 restarts the wait" is the raider's crack, and a heal restarts it too; an owner's repeat hits (encores) and a raider's parries never do, or one player tapping keeps an area cooling for good. The studio's draft notes for the plan say why the crack restarts it: it gives the defender a window (a Wonder's fall at "23 to 30 s at the very least": 23 s without the restart, 30 s with it). Decision 28, approved on 6 November, says the same of a god: "At most one hit counts every 5 s". This needs nothing from the producer.
- **The heal: as built it breaks the siege, and the fix changes the approved words.** A heal lands on any owner hit, even while the area is cooling (`areas.ts:127`), so one defender in reach holds a Lift, and in ab6c67c a god, against any number of raiders (M1). The first reading's one line, `if (a.cracked) out = ready ? 'heal' : 'encore';`, makes a heal need a ready hit, as the drafts had it. But decision 16's approved text says "an owner's hit", with no "ready", so this changes what the producer approved. And in the sieges it over-corrects: one raider against one owner, both tapping as fast, topples a defended 5-hit god in 374 of 400 three-minute sieges (median 61 s), and a 9-hit god in 120 of 200, where as built it fell in 0 of 400. The cause: at an uncracked top the owner's ready hits are encores that restart nothing, so the raider's first crack is always free. Letting the owner's ready hit at the top restart the wait as well still topples it in 345 of 400.
- **So the heal is for the producer**, with both ends in plain words: as built a defended god never falls; with a ready heal one raider topples it in about a minute. Before asking, the studio owes a middle rule run through the same sieges, so the question comes with a pick. The sieges model both sides tapping the target only, with splats as 9.7 s absences, not real rides or duels.
- **A tie, for the same rule.** Two breaths landing in one step resolve newest first ("1:claim 0:parry"). The player is bus 0, so under any quick-draw rule same-step ties go to the captains (n20).

## Findings

### Major

**M1. A defended stage 4 can never fall, and in ab6c67c neither can a defended god** (`areas.ts:126-127`; ab6c67c `areas.ts:195`).
- *What a player sees.* An owner who lands one hit within five seconds of each crack keeps their Lift for ever, without even looking: an owner tapping every 4.9 s holds for 10 minutes against 1 or 3 raiders. Tonight's Recess starts each camp with its god standing, so the match's main objective can't be won against a defender.
- *Evidence.* 1, 2 or 3 raiders hitting every 1.6 s for 10 minutes against an owner hitting 0.5, 2 or 4.9 s after each crack: stage 4 kept in all nine runs, 53 to 107 cracks, each healed. A 5-hit and a 9-hit god held 10 minutes against 3 raiders; with one raider, one owner and the pair rule's two splats, the god held in 200 of 200 sieges.
- *Splatting the owner* is the one way through, and it barely works on gods: on a square's Lift one 9.7 s absence took it in 67 of 200 sieges and two absences 20 s apart in 117 of 200; three raiders splatting the owner six times in two minutes toppled a god in 101 of 200.
- *Fix.* Waits on the producer's word on the heal (above). Then the chosen rule, and a siege row in `check.mjs` that fails if a defended top can't fall or falls in under a set time.

**M2. A breath at a locked bus flying straight can miss, and how often depends on the step size and on the target's direction** (`recess.ts:402`, `:407-413`; still in ab6c67c at `recess.ts:409`).
- *Why.* The lead aims one step past arrival (`lead = left + dt`); on its last step the breath moves a whole step past `arrive` and is compared with the bus at the end of that step.
- *What a player sees.* A glinting rival that never dodged, and the breath goes past it, which breaks "what glints is what your tap hits".
- *Evidence.* At 1/30 s with the arena's edge removed, a Nonstop target fleeing straight down the line of fire is missed 29.5 % of the time, at 45° off the line 17.5 %, crossing, at 135° or head-on 0 %. Fleeing at Nonstop: 23 of 200 at 150 m, rising to 77 of 200 at 450-490 m; breaths land up to 18.4 m off (the hit radius is 14 m) and fly at up to 556 m/s. At 1/20 s a fleeing Nonstop target is missed 79 % of the time, and even a Cruise one 18 %. At 1/60 s, 0 of 1,109. In the duels' own mix: 37 of 1,487 at Nonstop, 0 at Cruise and Express. `duels.mjs`'s glint row checks the target as the breath leaves, not where it lands.
- *Fix.* Lead to `arrive` and resolve the breath there (the first reading's `aimfix.mjs`): 0 misses in every case at every step, and the flick still hops clear. In 600 duels the misses with no hop behind them fall from 50 to 20; the other 20 come from the arena's edge and steering (m4). Add a row that checks where the breath lands, at 1/20 s.

**M3. A save this build can't fully read is dropped whole, and the next write erases it** (`areas.ts:221-241`, `:244-251`).
- *What a player sees.* After a rollback, Safari's stale cached build or one bad byte, their whole city's territory is gone at their next hit.
- *Evidence.* 50 areas saved with one record's stage set to 5: load returned false, and after the next change and write only 1 area was saved. One spoiled character that breaks the base64: 0 areas read. One that stays valid base64 is read without error and moves a record to a key no save had. `load()` returns false both when nothing is saved and when the save is unreadable, so a host can't tell them apart. Decode also takes a crack bit on a stage 2.
- *It has already happened.* ab6c67c reuses the stage byte's top bits for a crack count, still under `strugglebus.areas.v1`: 84b8270's crack bit reads there as 8 cracks, and ab6c67c's saves with a god or a cracked Lift are dropped whole by 84b8270 (0 areas read).
- *Fix.* As step 1 guards every paint store: keep what can be read, never overwrite a save that failed to read, and give the format a header with a version (m10) and a new key when it changes.

### Minor

**m4. The arena can't hold a Possessed bus** (`recess.ts:300-306`, `:67-68`). The edge turns at 1.2 rad/s ramped over 113 m, not enough to bring a 225 m/s bus round. In 600 duels 0.54 % of bus-steps were outside the circle, the farthest 990 m (240 m out) during a ride, and 621 of 9,084 hiccups painted outside the arena. A driver steering outward reaches 1,058 m and stays out 5.0 s at a time; a ride from 480 m steered out lands 6 of 8 hiccups outside, so a victim can choose to waste the hitter's paint. Still in ab6c67c.

**m5. Two hitters' supers collide** (`areas.ts:119-122`). Each SuperSplat numbers its supers from 1 (`SuperSplat.ts:191`, `:428`, `:473`), so a captain's first MEGA does nothing on an area the player's first MEGA counted: the captain's super 1, ready, 10 s later, gives `none`. And on one area super 7, then 8, then 7 again counts twice (claim, encore, up), so "a super counts once per area" breaks when two interleave.

**m6. Past a bar, the city-wide cap gives way** (`beat.ts:96-108`). A MEGA claiming 10 grey squares starts them by beat as {71: 2, 73: 2, 75: 6}: six on one beat, 3.47 s late. FACTIONS.md's 19-square MEGA starts 15 on one beat. STAGES.md 1.2's two limits, two changes a second and at most a bar late, can't both hold for it, and the code picks "a bar late": the studio should say which gives.

**m7. Captains never heal their own cracks** (`captain.ts:126-128`). In 600 duels, 0 heals by captains and 21 by the player's stand-in, so in Recess a captain's Lift falls to the player undefended under either reading of decision 16. ab6c67c's `captain.ts:126-133` still skips its own top and its own areas while cooling, against the studio's "captains defend their own heart first".

**m8. The "riding it out" payoff is paid with no ride, and half of it is spent under the stop arm** (`recess.ts:482`, `:487-497`). A Piñata ride lasts 0 s, yet the victim's super comes back and the hitter is marked. In every mode only 4 s of the 8 s mark can be used, because the 4 s stop arm runs at the same time.

**m9. The flick out of a white-hot breath has a window of about a quarter of a second** (`recess.ts:285-291`). As built it is 0.20 to 0.32 s; with M2's fix it is 0.233 s in every case, closing 0.075 to 0.1 s before arrival, so a thumb reacting more than about 0.23 to 0.26 s after the flash is late. A flick just before the white-hot shifts gear (Cruise to Express, Express to Nonstop) and the bus is still hit. The duels' player stand-in reacts in 0.07 to 0.13 s in a third of the duels, faster than a thumb. One for the phone to judge.

**m10. The crack doesn't record its attacker, and the save has no world number or rules version** (`areas.ts:52-53`, `:201-215`). The plan shows a crack in "veins of the attacker's colour", but when Inferno finishes an Atlantis crack nothing says whose crack it was; ab6c67c has no such field either. A header added later would be read by this decode as a record, which drops the save (M3). Add it before anything saves.

**m11. Two faction tables with two ranges** (`areas.ts:14`, `:188-191`, `:233`). Decode takes factions 1 to 15, `factions.ts` only 1 to `FACTION_ROOM` (7). A saved faction 9 reads back and `score()` returns 10 entries with NaN at index 9, in both commits; factions 6 and 7, which step 1's stores keep for a later build, give NaN too. After the merge, import `factions.ts`.

**m13. A ridden bus can lock and ride its rider** (`recess.ts:371`). Rivals can't lock a bus being ridden, but it can lock and ride them: 54 of 2,609 rides in 600 duels were by ridden buses, 43 of them on the bus riding it, and scripted, the hitter locked it 0 steps and was ridden back. The plan doesn't say whether it should. Raised from a nit.

### Nit

- **n12.** Hiccups' claims show a beat or two late (`recess.ts:250`, `:256`): 0 of 8 on their own beat. Passing `beat.time(i)` puts 2 of 8 on time; the city-wide cap pushes the rest a beat late after any one other change. Still in ab6c67c.
- **n14.** `encode()` of a painted San Francisco takes 16 to 18 ms and 200 KB, one blob a city. The garbage is small: 99 to 165 B a step for the step alone, 155 to 202 B with captains, after a longer warm-up 9 to 21 B and 250 to 400 B; `score()` 0.10 ms a call over 9,600 areas, so it shouldn't run every frame. The explicit allocations are `captain.ts:64` and `:66` and `recess.ts:347`: worth removing for the no-garbage rule. (The first reading's 2.4 KB a step didn't reproduce.)
- **n15.** Tests and docs: `check.mjs`'s "never past stage 4" row never gets past stage 1; `duels.mjs:243`'s score filter ends in `|| true` and reads ax/az as a place; the steering row guards "nobody loses the steering" but can't catch a yaw too weak to matter; TODO's "8.1 M bus-steps" is 8.1 M steps, 20.25 M bus-steps; BUILT.md's "one change a beat" is wrong, the cap allows two on one beat.
- **n16.** The sim's yaw is 0.699 rad/s at Express and 0.493 at Nonstop, where the real Flight gives 0.743 and 0.506; the sim also has no steering spring and a fixed 60 m/s Cruise.
- **n17.** A captain's hunt picks the nearest rival anywhere (`captain.ts:87-97`), past what the chase camera shows, against the file's header. (Its seeing the breath at its own bus, `:66`, is what the victim's screen shows, by FACTIONS.md's white-hot flash.)
- **n18.** If the host makes the Recess player a new player, every captain breath but one lesson bursts as sparkles and the demo never shows Possessed. A note for tonight's `?recess` host.
- **n19.** A repeated super on an area returns `none` (`areas.ts:120`), for the owner and for a rival, where STAGES.md:325 gives an encore or a parry.
- **n20.** Same-step ties resolve newest first, so they go to the captains (decision 16, above).
