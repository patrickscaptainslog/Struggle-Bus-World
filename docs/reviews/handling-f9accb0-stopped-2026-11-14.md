**M304. The Hose's and the Bomb's ids collide: a Bomb after a Hose hold (or a Hose after a Bomb) counts for nothing on the square, in silence.** `src/world/Lift.ts:725`
- *What happens.* On ?show (the Hose is awake with life, the Bomb with &bomb). The player's first still hold on the city pours on a rival's square S: at its sixth impact S cracks. Six seconds later, S ready, the player lets their first Bomb go on S. Nothing happens: no fall, no parry, no buzz, no claim-line pulse, and the Bomb's 3 to 4.5 s of belly is gone. The other way round too: the first Bomb cracks S, and the first Hose hold after it counts nothing however long it pours. It comes back whenever the n-th Bomb lands on a square whose last counted or parried super was the n-th Hose hold (or the reverse). 'Crack it with the Hose, finish it with a Bomb' on a first visit to the block is the plainest case.
- *Why.* Hose.ts and Bomb.ts each number their supers from a counter of their own, both from 1: `private serial = 0;` and `id: ++this.serial` (Hose.ts 166 and 374, Bomb.ts 93 and 228). Lift.heard hands the bare number on, `superId = b.super.id;`, and the rules take an equal number for the same super: `if (a.superId === o.superId) return { area: a, outcome: 'none', at: t };` (liftRules.ts 101; src/factions/areas.ts 212 has the same line, written when SuperSplat's one serial made every id). Lift.took returns at once on 'none', so nothing shows.
- *Evidence.* Ran, the real Lift.ts of f9accb0 bundled for Node with a stand-in city (scratchpad harness, t1.mjs), blasts shaped as Splat hands them to Lift.landed: 'T2 the first Hose hold, 8 impacts: crack -> 5/4/1 up 5 falling 0'; 'T2 then the first Bomb on it, 6 s later (ready true): took 0 call(s), counted +0, parried +0 -> 5/4/1 up 5 falling 0'; 'T2 the same moment, a Bomb with id 2: down -> 5/3/0 up 5 falling 5'; 'T2b the first Bomb, then the first Hose hold 6 s later, 24 impacts (2 s): crack ; then took 0 call(s) -> 5/4/1'. That both counters start at 1 is read from Hose.ts and Bomb.ts, not run (the real Hose and Bomb were not driven). No row, Node or browser, puts a Hose or a Bomb on the block (grep of tools/factions/take-*.mjs).
- *Fix.* One counter for every SuperTag id (a shared serial in Splat.ts that Hose, Bomb, Boom, Falls and SuperSplat draw from). Or, in the Lift's own file, make the id the kind's: `superId = b.super.id * 8 + KINDS.indexOf(b.super.kind)`, 'hose' and 'closer' sharing one. Whatever calls Areas.hit at the merge needs the same.

**M305. A bus that hovers in the square it is taking keeps that square from ever finishing its fall: every tap on it is parried for as long as the bus stays.** `src/world/Lift.ts:1041`

- *Evidence.* Ran (scratchpad harness, t2.mjs, the real Lift): 'T5b the bus parked under one house of the square, 30 s after the fall hit: 5/3/0 up 1 falling 1 ready never held frames 917'; 'T5b a tap on it then: parry'; 'T5b the bus gone, 3 s on: 5/3/0 up 0 falling 0'.
