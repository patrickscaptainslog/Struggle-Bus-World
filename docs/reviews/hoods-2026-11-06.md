## For the second account: the neighbourhood layer

Read at ab6c67c with the real files, and every line below checked again at bced508.

### Major

1. **A defended god never falls.** At stage 5, an owner's hit heals straight away, with no five-second wait (`areas.ts:207`, `if (own) out = a.cracks > 0 ? 'heal' : 'encore'`), and the heal sets `a.last`, so every attacker's next hit is a parry for 5 s. The review's `defend.mjs`:

   | Attackers | Defender away | Defender tapping every 1.6 s |
   |---|---|---|
   | 1 | toppled at 25.6 s | stands after 600 s: 94 cracks, 94 heals |
   | 2 | toppled at 22.4 s | stands after 600 s |
   | 3 | toppled at 21.3 s | stands after 600 s |
   | 5 | toppled at 20.5 s | stands after 600 s |

   Section 3 says at most one hit counts every 5 s, and the heal ignores that. `check.mjs` asserts the immediate heal, so the check encodes it. This is the heal in `recess-2026-11-06.md`. The studio's middle rule for decision 16 is being worked out tonight and goes to the producer in the morning. Until they rule, leave it.

### Minor

2. **Ties at exactly half go to the highest faction.** `standing()` sets `holder = f` for every faction at half (`areas.ts:306`), so Inferno always wins a 32 to 32 split over Rainbow.
3. **Recess's score with a real table.** `Recess.score()` counts `realm.of(a.key) !== -1` (`recess.ts:534`). On the camps that's the match. With a real table it's the whole city, not the 1.5 km circle.
4. **A weak test.** The "lone captain ... the score 36" row (`duels.mjs:276`) passes on squares alone: the scores were 153 to 160.
5. **Short rings.** `ring()` counts only ring squares inside the neighbourhood. On the fixed files, 9 of San Francisco's gods, 1 of Amsterdam's and 2 of London's need 4 to 7 lifted squares, not 8. C18's O20 asks what a short ring means, with a pick.
6. **A god in a contested Recess never comes.** `duels.mjs` printed "0 of 750 matches raise a god", as `BUILT.md` reports. On the real table it's harder still: no three neighbourhoods of 15 to 40 squares fit whole in a 1.5 km circle, and the tightest, `FACTIONS.md`'s own Alamo Square, Hayes Valley and the Lower Haight, reach 835 m from their middle. C18's O17 asks, with a pick.

### Nits

7. `leader()`'s comment says a god's heart counts "its 5" (`areas.ts:330`). With `godPoints` 0 it counts 4, `areaTune.top`.
8. `decode()` accepts factions up to 15 (`areas.ts:392`), but `held` and `score` have 6 slots (`:300`, `:345`), so a later build's faction 6 gives NaN in `score`.

### What checks out, on the real files

- A solo god rose by the rules on 112 of 112 San Francisco neighbourhoods, 6 of 6 in Amsterdam and 6 of 6 in London (the fix's `fill.mjs`, on ab6c67c taken with `git archive`).
- At 3184cf5's 105 gods: topple hits run 5 to 9 by size, and god heights run 248 to 450 m, median 304 m.
- The god rises only at the heart, cracks are on the heart, tagged squares stop at stage 3, and hiccups claim only at stage 1.
- `Neighbourhoods` takes the loader's `HoodsTable` as it is.
